### [1] [Untrusted Authors, Trusted Answers: A Calculus of Fidelity-Graded Translations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14137)
*Christoph Kirsch*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于保真度分级的翻译验证演算，将翻译验证从孤立的单次验证扩展到多语言、多目标、多路径的翻译图场景。该演算通过可逐程序检查的交换方块组合、最弱链环的保真度分级、独立推导路径的一致性验证等机制，构建了端到端的正确性定理。该演算在Lean 4中形式化，并在hurdy-gurdy平台上实现（13种语言、13个翻译对），所有代码和论文大部分由LLM生成，人类贡献仅在于架构设计。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有认证编译和翻译验证方法都将翻译视为孤立事件，无法处理多语言、多目标、多路径构成的翻译图场景。研究者希望建立一种能够在翻译图级别进行正确性推理的框架，让不同保真度等级的翻译能够协同工作，并通过架构而非作者身份保证质量。

Method: 提出保真度分级翻译演算：(1) 语言对之间通过可逐程序检查的交换方块（commuting squares）进行组合（pasting）；(2) 保真度等级按最弱链环原则组合，每次运行通过内联检查重新建立，独立推导路径之间的一致性可超越原有等级；(3) 端到端定理揭示了非对称性：携带见证的答案在源端自认证，而全称答案则需要等级、分支和证书来证明其成本合理性。核心演算在Lean 4中机械化实现。

Result: 在hurdy-gurdy平台实现了13种语言和13个翻译对（围绕两个推理中心构建），所有翻译对由独立的LLM代理无监督编写，架构交叉检查是唯一的语义门控。报告了联合覆盖率、分支一致性、基于合规的基准测试（含机器推导的ground truth）、见证重放、认证不可达性和门控逃逸率实验。

Conclusion: 该架构本身是论文的核心贡献，所有代码和论文由LLM生成。门控机制承诺可扩展性：任何人都可以通过LLM、代理或手工贡献翻译对，由架构而非作者身份决定是否接纳。该工作展示了LLM生成代码在架构约束下实现正确性的可行路径。

Abstract: Verified translation has two well-studied extremes: prove the translator once (certified compilation), or validate each run of one translator (translation validation). Both treat a single translation in isolation. We study translations as a graph -- many source languages, several reasoning targets, multiple independently built routes -- where the honest answer to "is this translation correct?" differs from edge to edge. We present a calculus of fidelity-graded translations: pairs of languages close commuting squares that are checkable per program and compose by pasting; declared fidelity grades compose by weakest link, are re-established per run by inline checking, and are exceeded by agreement between independently derived routes; and an end-to-end theorem isolates a fundamental asymmetry -- witness-carrying answers are self-certifying at the source, while universal answers are where grades, branches, and certificates earn their cost. The compositional core is mechanized in Lean 4.
  The calculus is implemented in hurdy-gurdy, a platform of 13 languages and 13 pairs around two reasoning hubs, built as a two-directional experiment in LLM-generated correctness: independent LLM agents wrote every pair, largely unsupervised, with the architecture's cross-checks as the only semantic gate, and the platform's intended player is itself an LLM. All code, and most of this paper, is LLM-generated; the human contribution is the architecture. The same gate is the intended growth model: hurdy-gurdy scales in language support through pairs contributed by anyone -- with LLMs, with agents, or by hand -- admitted by architecture, not authorship. We report conjoined coverage, branch agreement, a compliance-derived benchmark with machine-derived ground truth, witness replay, certified unreachability, and escape-rate experiments for the gate.

### [2] [Volition Elicitation: Operational Semantics for People and Their Machines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14138)
*Ehud Shapiro*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文定义了vGLP编程语言，通过人的意志守卫子句扩展了GLP，并以其操作语义实现了通信意志代理系统。通过AI将意志守卫的多智能体原子事务生成vGLP程序，并映射为用户界面，在物理智能手机上部署了社交图、社交网络和货币等应用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前最普遍的分布式系统包含人和个人设备（如智能手机），计算由人的意志驱动（如支付、交友等）。需要一种能够同时考虑机器状态和个人意志的形式化规范语言，来建模此类系统。

Method: 定义了vGLP编程语言，扩展了GLP以支持意志守卫子句；其操作语义作为通信意志代理的实例实现，要求用户主动意愿意志守卫子句的归约；通过AI从意志守卫多智能体原子事务生成vGLP程序，再映射为用户界面构件。

Result: 在草根社交图、社交网络和货币三个平台上验证了方法：每个平台由AI生成的vGLP程序实现，并部署为物理智能手机上的一个完整应用。

Conclusion: 正确实现必须 elicit 人的意志——即"搞清楚人脑子里想什么"是UI的唯一理由，通过标准构件实现；该框架成功将形式化规范落地到人机融合的分布式系统。

Abstract: The most prevalent distributed systems today include people and their personal machines (smartphones). In such systems, computations are driven by people's volitions: a payment when a person wishes to pay someone, befriending when two people wish to become friends, etc. Volition-Guarded Multiagent Atomic Transactions were proposed as an abstract specification language for such systems, in which each agent consists of a person and their machine, and a transaction can be guarded by both the machine states and the personal volitions of its participating agents. Here, we define the programming language volition-guarded GLP (vGLP), which extends GLP with volition-guarded clauses, and define its operational semantics as an instance of Communicating Volitional Agents. As the semantics requires the person to will a volition-guarded clause reduction, a correct implementation must elicit the person's volitions: finding out ``what's in the person's head'' is the sole rationale for the UI, which is realised accordingly by standard constructs.
  We demonstrate the approach on the grassroots social graph, social network, and currencies: each platform is a vGLP program, generated by AI from volition-guarded multiagent atomic transactions; the implementation of vGLP, also created by AI, then maps its volition-guarded clauses into the user-interface constructs, resulting in a single working app deployed on a physical smartphone.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: POPL'24提出的双面类型系统扩展了传统类型系统，允许对任意项（而非仅变量）进行类型假设，并能得出多个类型赋值结论。本文旨在从命题即类型（Curry-Howard同构）的角度探索双面类型系统的逻辑基础，建立其与双边逻辑（bilateral logic）之间的对应关系。

Method: 本文通过构造性方法，设计了两个基于λ演算的双面类型系统：(1) 2λInt，对应Wansing的双边逻辑2Int；(2) 2λInt^~，在2Int基础上扩展了Nelson强否定。进一步，将双面类型系统扩展到高阶逻辑，提出2λHOL，作为Guevers' λHOL的扩展。

Result: 证明了2λHOL的表达充分性（expressive adequacy）和一致性（consistency），并验证了它同时满足存在性（existence property）及其对偶性质。

Conclusion: 双面类型系统不仅具有类型理论上的扩展能力，还具有坚实的双边逻辑基础，可以通过命题即类型范式与特定的逻辑系统建立精确对应关系。

Abstract: Two-sided type systems, introduced in POPL'24, are an extension of the traditional notion of type system that allows for stating and deriving typing judgements in which (a) assumptions can be made about the types of arbitrary terms and not only variables, and (b) conclusions can be made about any number of type assignments, and not exactly one. In this work, we investigate the logical foundations of two-sided type systems in the sense of the propositions-as-types paradigm. We introduce new two-sided type systems 2$λ$Int and 2$λ$Int$^{\sim}$ that correspond with Wansing's bilateral logic 2Int and its extension with Nelson's strong negation respectively. Going beyond the propositional case, we introduce 2$λ$HOL as an extension of Guevers' $λ$HOL, and we show its expressive adequacy, its consistency and that it satisfies both the existence property and its dual.

### [4] [A Fast Quantitative Analyzer for NetKAT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14420)

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了基于加权NetKAT（wNetKAT）的快速网络定量属性分析器，通过加权符号数据包程序（wSPPs）紧凑表示语义，并设计了Kleene星号的专用算法，支持多目标权衡分析。在Lean中形式化，Rust实现，在布尔可达性、概率分析等场景下显著优于现有工具，并通过Fat-tree与Jellyfish拓扑的案例研究验证了多目标设计时分析能力。

A case study comparing Fat-tree and Jellyfish data-center topologies shows the framework supports multi-objective design-time analysis.

### [5] [Simply Typed Reverse-Mode AD with Variants: Denotational Correctness via Idempotent Completion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14453)
*Fernando Lucatelli Nunes,Diogo Simm,Matthijs Vákár*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文证明：反向模式自动微分中，变体类型所需的依赖余切空间可以通过在普通非依赖目标类型中嵌入余切纤维并使用主索引幂等算子进行选择来等价表示。通过Karoubi完备化，建立了常族模型与依赖族模型之间的等价性，从而将依赖语义和带投影的简单类型语义统一为同一种指称变换的两种等价表述。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 变体类型（variant types）使得反向模式自动微分的传统简单类型表示（每个源类型对应单一余切类型）失效，因为有效余切空间依赖于运行时分支选择。现有正确性结果使用依赖类型的族模型，但能否在不引入依赖类型的情况下表示这种依赖关系是一个开放问题。

Method: 提出在普通非依赖目标类型中嵌入所有余切纤维，通过主索引的幂等算子（idempotent）选择有效纤维。从范畴论角度，这等价于将常族模型（constant-family model）进行Karoubi完备化。进一步证明：对于Cauchy完备的范畴C，Karoubi完备化后的常族模型等价于依赖族模型当且仅当每个κ-小族都有公共的retract宿主。

Abstract: Reverse-mode automatic differentiation is commonly given a denotational account in which each source type has a single cotangent type. The cotangent fibres of each source type are embedded in a common ambient type, and a primal-indexed idempotent selects the valid fibre. Splitting the generated idempotents recovers the established dependent semantics. Thus dependent cotangent families and simply typed ambient cotangents equipped with projectors are equivalent presentations of the same denotational transformation.

### [6] [Mech: Mechanised Choreographic Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15174)
*Xueying Qin,Marco Peressotti,Fabrizio Montesi*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文在Lean 4中提出了Mech——一种支持知识选择分支、一般递归和非确定性选择等丰富特性的编舞编排编程形式化系统，通过新的语义定义和代数定律，首次实现了端点投影的完备性和正确性的机械化证明，是目前最全面的编舞编排机械化理论。

  <summary>Details</summary>

Abstract: Choreographic programming (CP) is a programming paradigm for the correct-by-construction development of concurrent and distributed systems: programmers write the intended overall behaviour of a system from a global perspective in a choreography, which is then automatically compiled into communicating endpoint programs by a procedure known as endpoint projection (EPP).   Fulfilling this promise becomes delicate for expressive CP languages. We address this by uncovering new algebraic laws for choreographies, the operators used in their semantics, EPP, and their combinations. Using our development, we prove completeness and soundness of EPP and derive communication safety and deadlock-freedom for projected networks, yielding the most extensive mechanised theory of CP to date.

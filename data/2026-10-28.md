### [1] [Proof Repair and (Co)algebra Equivalences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36669)
*E. G Plante,Eric Paul,Cosmo Viola,G. A. Kavvos,Talia Ringer*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 论文首次为类型论证明修复（proof repair）建立理论基础，指出证明修复算法的核心组件"configuration"本质上是一个代数同构，并通过对偶化推广到余归纳类型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 依赖关系发生破坏性变更时维护形式化证明是证明工程的主要挑战；现有证明修复研究多从算法视角理解，缺乏理论视角，存在开放问题。

Method: 针对数据类型变更导致的类型论证明修复给出奠基性处理；形式化并证明"configuration"等价于代数同构这一关键洞见，并取其对偶得到余归纳类型上的证明修复。

Result: 回答了先前工作中的开放问题，并证明对偶表示可导出余归纳类型的证明修复；所有结果均在 Agda 中形式化。

Conclusion: 证明修复可通过代数同构获得统一的理论刻画，并可自然扩展到余归纳情形，为证明修复提供理论基础。

Abstract: Maintaining formal proofs in response to breaking changes in their dependencies is a major challenge of proof engineering. Proof repair is a kind of proof automation that lessens the burden of this challenge by automatically adapting proofs in response to those changes. While proof repair is well understood through an algorithmic lens, it has, until now, been poorly understood through a theoretical lens. We address this gap by giving the first foundational treatment of proof repair, focusing in particular on type-theoretic proof repair in response to changes in datatypes. Our key insight is that the core component of a proof repair algorithm---a ``configuration''---is exactly an algebra isomorphism. We formalize and prove this insight, answering open questions from prior work. We then take this one step further and show that, by taking the dual of this representation, we obtain proof repair over coinductive types. All of our results are formalized in Agda.

### [2] [Polymorphic First-Class Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36798)
*Jinhao Tan,Jam Kabeer Ali Khan,Bruno C. d. S. Oliveira*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出 $F_E$：一个带一等环境的多态演算，以小步环境语义保守扩展 System F，完全消除代换，证明类型健全性、规范化、可判定性与保守性，并机化验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有一等环境演算多局限于简单类型，未推广到 System F 与多态；而一等环境可支持一等模块、环境片段动态组合与沙箱化等应用，因此需要将一等环境与多态结合。

Method: 设计 $F_E$：项抽象与类型抽象求值为闭包，box 在局部环境下运行代码，box 类型暴露项与类型的局部类型假设；引入 manifest 类型与双侧类型等价，承担类型级实例化与环境中透明类型组件的双重角色。元理论以实例化引理取代代换引理；给出显式类型传递版本（遵循 System F）与隐式类型变体（契合多态函数式语言语义），全部元理论在 Rocq 中机化。

Result: 证明类型健全性、规范化、可判定性、类型等价的标准性质，以及相对 System F 的保守性。

Conclusion: $F_E$ 为一等环境提供了多态基础，支撑带动态组合与沙箱化的多态一等模块，其无代换的实例化式元理论为后续语言设计奠定基础。

Abstract: First-class environments expose runtime environments as ordinary values and support a substitution-free, environment-based operational semantics. They also support applications such as first-class modules, dynamic composition of environment fragments, and sandboxing via local environments. Existing environment-based calculi, however, are mostly studied in a simply typed setting and stop short of System F and polymorphism.
  We present $F_E$, a polymorphic calculus with first-class environments that conservatively extends System F with a small-step environment semantics. $F_E$ eliminates all forms of substitution in System F. Term and type abstractions evaluate to closures, boxes run code under local environments, and box types expose the local typing assumptions of both. Manifest types, together with a two-sided type equivalence, play a dual role: they model type-level instantiation, and enable transparent type components in environments and modules. We prove type soundness, normalization, decidability, and standard properties of type equivalence, and conservativity over System F. The metatheory of $F_E$ is based on instantiation lemmas rather than substitution lemmas. This foundation supports polymorphic first-class modules with dynamic composition and sandboxing. We study $F_E$ in two forms. Our main presentation is an explicit, type-passing calculus that follows conventional System F. We also develop an implicitly-typed variant aligned with the semantics of polymorphic functional languages. All metatheory is mechanized in the Rocq theorem prover.

</details>


### [3] [Abstract Lipschitz Continuity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37006)
*Marco Campion,Isabella Mastroeni,Michele Pasqua,Caterina Urban*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出抽象Lipschitz连续性（ALC），区分定性（性质保留）与定量（度量接近）两类近似，并给出参数化的可靠演绎系统以按程序语法归纳证明ALC。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 既有的抽象解释（上闭包算子）刻画性质近似，而程序分析中的距离常用度量推广形式即前度量刻画定量近似；两者未被显式区分，导致难以精确刻画若干外延性质（如偏完备性、抽象非干扰、抽象鲁棒性）之间的关系，也缺乏按语法结构归纳推理程序语义性质的方法。

Method: 定义ALC为函数（尤其程序语义）的外延性质：输入性质差异有界则输出性质差异按比例有界。显式区分基于性质的上闭包算子式定性近似与前度量式定量近似，并在该框架下把ALC与已有概念建立联系；进而给出一个对性质近似与前度量参数化的、按程序语法归纳的可靠演绎系统，将程序整体的ALC证明归约为基本命令的证明与显式副作用条件。

Result: 证明ALC是抽象解释中偏完备性的充分条件、语言安全中偏抽象非干扰的充分条件，且当Lipschitz常数为零时是机器学习中抽象鲁棒性的充分条件；演绎系统通过显式副作用条件刻画了ALC在顺序复合、非确定性选择与迭代下的可组合性。

Conclusion: 显式分离定性与定量两类近似使ALC既能统一刻画多种既有外延性质，又能支持结构归纳式的程序证明，为程序语义的定量—定性联合分析提供了参数化、可组合的推理框架。

Abstract: We introduce Abstract Lipschitz Continuity (ALC), an extensional property of functions, in particular of program semantics, requiring that bounded differences between properties of two inputs yield proportionally bounded differences between properties of the corresponding outputs. ALC explicitly discerns between two complementary forms of approximation: property-based (or qualitative) approximations, which retain a property of the data and are formalized by upper closure operators as in abstract interpretation, and quantitative approximations, which measure the closeness of data and are formalized by pre-metrics, a weakening of metrics that covers the distances arising in program analysis. Keeping the two forms of approximation explicit has two main benefits. First, it allows us to precisely relate ALC to established extensional properties: ALC is a sufficient condition for partial completeness in abstract interpretation, for partial abstract non-interference in language-based security and, with a null Lipschitz constant, for abstract robustness in machine learning. Second, it supports reasoning about ALC of program semantics by induction on the syntax of programs: we present a sound deductive system, parametric in the property-based approximations and in the pre-metric of interest, that reduces a proof of ALC for a program to proofs for its basic commands and to some explicit side conditions, which isolate what makes ALC compositional across sequential composition, non-deterministic choice and iteration.

### [4] [Preserving Separation Logic Specifications through Compilation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37244)
*Carmine Abate,Kleio Liotati,František Farka,Sebastian Ertel*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文研究"最弱前置条件保持编译"(WP-preserving compilation)：编译器把源程序翻译为目标程序时，源程序在分离逻辑中满足的最弱前置条件可传递给编译后的程序。作者提出基于交互树(interaction trees)的资源感知模拟证明技术，并将其应用于从结构化命令式语言到类汇编语言的编译器，支持模块化验证与链接。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 编译器正确性通常表述为语义保持，但语义保持本身不为编译后代代码提供分离逻辑意义上的逻辑接口，因为它没有显式关联源与目标资源及断言。因此在分离逻辑视角下，需要更强的编译正确性概念，使源程序的逻辑规范（最弱前置条件）能够迁移到编译结果，从而支持对编译后代码的局部推理。

Method: 核心技术是交互树上的资源感知模拟(resource-aware simulation)：将对应的源事件与目标事件所需的资源相互关联，并在目标操作（如寄存器访问等无源对应物的操作）中跟踪所有权。方法先针对封闭程序，再扩展至带模块与函数调用的场景；扩展设定中每个模块可在源逻辑中独立验证并独立编译，其规范在编译后保持，并在链接时保持。

Result: 建立了从结构化命令式语言到类汇编语言的WP保持编译性质：封闭程序情形与带模块、函数调用的扩展情形均获得证明；模块化情形下源逻辑规范在单独编译与链接后均得以保持。全部结果在Rocq中机械化验证。

Conclusion: 分离逻辑规范可作为编译代码的逻辑接口，WP保持编译为编译后代码的局部推理提供了可行路径；资源感知的交互树模拟是实现该性质的有效证明技术，并可与模块化编译与链接相兼容。

Abstract: Program specifications in separation logic describe ownership of resources and serve as logical interfaces for local reasoning. Compiler correctness, expressed as semantics preservation, does not by itself supply such an interface for compiled code, because it does not explicitly relate source and target resources and assertions. We investigate WP-preserving compilation: when a compiler translates a source program, the weakest preconditions that the program satisfies in a separation logic carry over to the compiled program. Our proof technique consists of a resource-aware simulation over interaction trees: it relates the resources required by corresponding source and target events, and tracks ownership through target operations, such as register accesses, that have no source counterpart. We apply the approach to a compiler from a structured imperative language to an assembly-like language, first for closed programs and then extended with modules and function calls. In the extended setting, each module can be verified in the source logic and compiled separately; its specifications carry over to the compiled module and are preserved when compiled modules are linked. All results are mechanized in Rocq.

</details>

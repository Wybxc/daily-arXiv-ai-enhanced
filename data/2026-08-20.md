### [1] [MicroPython and CircuitPython: Pythons Quiet Takeover of IoT and Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18160)
*Sayed Mahbub Hasan Amiri,Atiar Zahan*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文研究了MicroPython和CircuitPython是否正在悄然改变嵌入式系统领域，通过混合方法（GitHub/Stack Overflow/Google Trends数据分析、案例研究、以及ESP32和树莓派Pico上的原始基准测试）发现：Python不会取代C/C++，但正在成为嵌入式系统默认的原型和教学语言，尽管I/O性能比C慢10-20倍、内存使用高4-6倍，但对常见传感器和网络任务仍足够。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Python已成为软件和数据科学领域的主导语言，但嵌入式系统因性能和内存限制一直依赖C/C++。MicroPython和CircuitPython的出现可能改变这一格局，作者旨在通过证据驱动的分析来检验这种"嵌入式系统被Python悄悄接管"的趋势是否真实存在。

Method: 采用混合研究方法：（1）量化分析GitHub、Stack Overflow和Google Trends的生态增长数据；（2）从Hackster.io、Hackaday.io和Adafruit Learning System整理实际应用案例；（3）在ESP32和树莓派Pico上运行原创基准测试，对比MicroPython、CircuitPython和Arduino C++在GPIO、I2C、SPI、Wi-Fi和内存使用方面的性能表现。

Result: 生态指标显示持续增长，MicroPython支持超过200块开发板，CircuitPython支持超过400块。基准测试显示Python的I/O速度比C慢10-20倍，内存使用高4-6倍，但性能足以满足常见传感器和网络任务。案例研究展示了在智能家居、机器人、可穿戴设备、农业和专业原型设计中的成功部署，教育领域是主要的采纳驱动因素。

Conclusion: Python并未取代C/C++，而是正在成为嵌入式系统的默认原型设计语言和教学语言。随着硬件性能持续提升、工具链改进和标准化推进，这一趋势将可能进一步深化。文章为开发者、教育者和技术决策者提供了均衡且基于证据的洞察。

Abstract: Background: Python has become the dominant language in software and data science, yet embedded systems have remained tied to C/C++ due to performance and memory constraints. MicroPython and CircuitPython are changing this by bringing Python to microcontrollers, lowering barriers for IoT and robotics development. Aim: This article examines whether these platforms are achieving a quiet takeover of embedded systems, focusing on ecosystem growth, practical applications, performance trade-offs, educational adoption, and prospects. Methods: A mixed-methods design was used, including quantitative analysis of GitHub, Stack Overflow, and Google Trends data; curation of case studies from Hackster.io, Hackaday.io, and the Adafruit Learning System; and original benchmarks on ESP32 and Raspberry Pi Pico comparing MicroPython, CircuitPython, and Arduino C++ across GPIO, I2C, SPI, Wi-Fi, and memory usage. Results: Metrics show sustained growth, with MicroPython supporting over 200 boards and CircuitPython over 400. Benchmarks reveal 10-20 times slower I/O and four to six times higher memory use than C, but performance remains adequate for common sensor and network tasks. Case studies demonstrate successful deployment in home automation, robotics, wearables, agriculture, and professional prototyping. Education emerges as a primary adoption driver. Conclusions: Python is not replacing C/C++; rather, it is becoming the default prototyping and educational language for embedded systems. Continued hardware improvements, better tooling, and standardization will likely deepen this trend. The article offers balanced, evidence-based insights for developers, educators, and technology decision-makers.

### [2] [Compiling WebAssembly Concolic Execution with Staging, Continuations, and Snapshots (Extended Version)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18327)
*Dinghong Zhong,Alexander Bai,Mikail Khan,Guannan Wei*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种新的混合型concolic执行方法，通过定义式concolic解释器并结合staging编译技术消除解释开销，同时利用CPS风格实现分支点的执行快照复用，避免从程序入口重新执行。该方法在WebAssembly上实现为GenWasym系统，相比解释器方案WASP取得29.4倍加速，结合启发式快照复用时可达44.9倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有concolic执行引擎面临两难选择：解释器方案实现简单但解释开销大，插桩方案避免了开销问题却需要为每个新输入从头重新执行整个程序。作者希望同时获得两种方案的优势——既保留解释器实现的简洁性，又消除解释开销并避免重复执行。

Method: 从目标语言的具象语义出发，首先构建定义式（definitional）concolic解释器，再通过staging（分层编译）技术将解释器编译掉以消除解释开销。staged解释器采用续延传递风格（CPS），使得在分支点可以捕获执行快照，在探索替代路径时从快照恢复执行而无需从头重跑。此外，为减少快照复用本身的额外开销，开发了启发式策略，仅在预期有收益时才启用快照复用。该方法实例化为WebAssembly的concolic执行编译器GenWasym。

Result: 在184个基准测试上，仅使用staging的GenWasym相比基于解释器的WASP平均获得29.4倍加速；结合启发式快照复用后进一步提升至44.9倍平均加速。

Conclusion: 通过将定义式解释器与staging编译、CPS风格快照复用及启发式选择相结合，可以在保持解释器实现简洁性的同时获得接近插桩方案的执行效率，有效弥合了两种concolic执行实现策略之间的性能鸿沟。

Abstract: Concolic execution is a variant of symbolic execution that runs a program simultaneously with concrete and symbolic inputs. It records the symbolic constraints encountered along a concrete execution path, then solves those constraints to generate inputs that explore new paths. Existing concolic engines generally follow one of two implementation strategies: Interpreter-based systems are comparatively simple to build but incur substantial interpretation overhead, while instrumentation-based systems avoid this overhead but typically re-execute the program from the beginning for each new input.
  In this paper, we develop a new approach that achieves the best of both worlds. Starting from the concrete semantics of the target language, we first develop a definitional concolic interpreter and stage it to compile away interpretation overhead while retaining the simplicity of an interpretation-based implementation. By expressing the staged interpreter in continuation-passing style, we can capture execution snapshots at branch points and resume from them when exploring alternative paths, avoiding repeated execution from the program entry. Because snapshot-reuse can itself incur overhead, we further develop a heuristic that favors snapshot-reuse only when it is expected to be beneficial. We instantiate this approach for WebAssembly and implement it in a new concolic-execution compiler GenWasym. Across 184 benchmarks, GenWasym with staging alone achieves a $29.4\times$ average speedup over the interpreter-based WASP; heuristic snapshot-reuse further increases the speedup to $44.9\times$.

### [3] [Towards a Deductive Verification Infrastructure for Weighted Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18971)
*Emma Ahrens,Samuel Rode,Philipp Schröer,Joost-Pieter Katoen*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个基于加权程序（weighted programs）的演绎验证框架，使用加权断言语言和中间验证语言，将归约义务（lower- and upper-bound obligations）内嵌到有序结构中，并通过量化消去过程实现自动化验证。原型工具Caesar在多个案例（如概率排队成本、递归数据库溯源、网络清关边界、CAS计数器无锁性）上得到验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 加权程序通过从幺半群-模（monoid-module）或半环中提取迹权重（trace weights），为多种定量和符号模型提供了统一的编程语法。然而，缺乏一个系统的演绎验证框架来对这些程序进行组合式推理，特别是关于权重上下界的验证条件的表达与自动化处理。

Method: 提出一个基于加权断言语言和中间验证语言的演绎验证框架。权重域采用带有蕴含（implication）和余蕴含（coimplication）的有序结构，使验证条件能够在内部表达下界和上界义务。证明了核心命令的可靠翻译，以及应用于过程调用和循环的各种证明规则的可复用编码。此外，证明了断言语言的量化消去过程的可靠性以促进自动化。实现了Caesar验证器原型。

Result: 在Caesar验证器中实现原型，并在多个案例研究中验证：概率排队成本、带循环依赖的递归数据库溯源、任意规模网络的清关边界、以及CAS计数器的无锁性形式化语言推理。

Conclusion: 该框架成功地将加权程序的弱前置权重语义（weakest-preweighting semantics）扩展为可组合的演绎验证系统，通过有序结构与量化消去实现了自动化，证明了其在多种定量和符号模型上的通用性与实用性。

Abstract: Weighted programs extend guarded commands with trace weights drawn from a semiring, or more generally a monoid-module. Varying this algebra gives one programmatic syntax for a variety of quantitative and symbolic models. Weakest-preweighting semantics provides a compositional basis for reasoning about those programs.
  We present a deductive verification framework based on a weighted assertion language and an intermediate verification language. Its weight domains are ordered structures with implication and coimplication, which let verification conditions express lower- and upper-bound obligations internally. We prove sound translations of core commands and reusable encodings for various proof rules applying to procedure calls and loops. To facilitate automation, we prove soundness of a quantifier elimination procedure for our assertion language. A prototype in the Caesar verifier checks case studies for probabilistic queueing costs, recursive database provenance with cyclic dependencies, clearance bounds for networks of arbitrary size, and formal-language reasoning about lock-freedom of a compare-and-swap counter.

</details>

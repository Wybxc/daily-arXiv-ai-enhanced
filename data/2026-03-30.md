### [1] [ExVerus: Verus Proof Repair via Counterexample Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25810)
*Jun Yang,Yuechun Sun,Yi Wu,Rodrigo Caridad,Yongwei Yuan,Jianan Yao,Shan Lu,Kexin Pei*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: EXVERUS：基于反例引导的LLM形式验证框架，通过反例提供行为反馈，指导LLM生成归纳不变量来提升证明准确性


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有LLM形式验证方法将证明生成视为静态的端到端预测，依赖有限的验证器反馈且无法访问具体程序行为，导致证明能力受限

Method: 提出EXVERUS框架，当证明失败时自动生成并验证反例，然后引导LLM将反例泛化为归纳不变量来阻止这些失败

Result: EXVERUS在证明准确性、鲁棒性和token效率方面显著优于最先进的基于提示的Verus证明生成器

Conclusion: 反例引导的框架能够有效增强LLM在形式验证中的推理能力，通过行为反馈机制提升证明生成质量

Abstract: Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown promising results in automating formal verification. However, existing approaches treat proof generation as a static, end-to-end prediction over source code, relying on limited verifier feedback and lacking access to concrete program behaviors. We present EXVERUS, a counterexample-guided framework that enables LLMs to reason about proofs using behavioral feedback via counterexamples. When a proof fails, EXVERUS automatically generates and validates counterexamples, and then guides the LLM to generalize them into inductive invariants to block these failures. Our evaluation shows that EXVERUS significantly improves proof accuracy, robustness, and token efficiency over the state-of-the-art prompting-based Verus proof generator.

### [2] [On the computational complexity of JavaScript regex matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26139)
*Victor Deng,Aurèle Barrière,Clément Pit-Claudel*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该论文证明了JavaScript正则表达式匹配的复杂度：完整版是PSPACE-hard和OptP-hard；无下界量词版是PSPACE-complete；无下界量词和无环视版是OptP-complete。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尽管正则表达式被广泛使用，但现代正则表达式匹配的复杂度类别尚未被充分理解。先前工作证明了带反向引用和环视的正则表达式匹配是PSPACE-complete的，但证明未机械化且应用于抽象正则语言。本文旨在澄清JavaScript正则表达式的复杂度问题。

Method: 使用形式化方法，在Rocq证明助手中机械化核心证明。通过构造归约证明JavaScript正则表达式匹配的复杂度下界，并分析不同语言子集的复杂度类别。

Result: 1. JavaScript正则表达式匹配是PSPACE-hard和OptP-hard的，即使没有负向环视。
2. 无下界量词（即重复次数最小值为零的量词）的JavaScript正则表达式匹配是PSPACE-complete的。
3. 无下界量词且无环视的JavaScript正则表达式匹配是OptP-complete的。

Conclusion: 该研究首次形式化证明了JavaScript正则表达式匹配的精确复杂度类别，填补了理论理解上的空白。机械化证明增强了结果的可靠性，为编程语言设计和静态分析工具提供了理论基础。

Abstract: Despite widespread use, the complexity class of modern regular expression matching was not well-understood. Previous work proved that regular expression matching with backreferences and lookarounds was PSPACE-complete, but the proof was not mechanized and applied to an abstract regex language. This paper clarifies the question for JavaScript regular expressions. In this paper, we prove the following new results, with most core proofs mechanized in the Rocq proof assistant. We prove that JavaScript regex matching is indeed PSPACE-hard, even without negative lookarounds, and OptP-hard as well; that JavaScript regex matching without lower-bounded quantifiers (i.e. quantifiers with a non-zero minimum number of repetitions) is PSPACE-complete; and that JavaScript regex matching without lower-bounded quantifiers and without lookarounds is OptP-complete.

### [3] [SuperDP: Differential Privacy Refutation via Supermartingales](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26215)
*Krishnendu Chatterjee,Ehsan Kafshdar Goharshady,Đorđe Žikelić*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出了一种自动化形式化反驳ε-差分隐私的新方法，利用概率程序分析中的上期望超鞅和下期望次鞅，通过搜索输入对和输出上的非负函数来反驳ε-DP


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 差分隐私的形式化验证和反驳具有挑战性且容易出错，现有方法在自动化程度、适用性、完备性保证等方面存在不足，需要开发更强大的反驳方法

Method: 通过搜索一对输入和输出上的非负函数，利用上期望超鞅和下期望次鞅构建ε-DP反驳的证明规则，该方法完全自动化且适用于离散和连续分布的随机机制

Result: 原型工具SuperDP在实验中表现出优于现有技术的性能，能够反驳文献中多个具有挑战性的ε-DP实例，包括先前方法无法处理的情况

Conclusion: 该方法首次实现了完全自动化、适用于离散和连续分布、提供可靠性保证和半完备性保证的ε-DP反驳，是差分隐私形式化分析的重要进展

Abstract: Differential privacy (DP) has established itself as one of the standards for ensuring privacy of individual data. However, reasoning about DP is a challenging and error-prone task, hence methods for formal verification and refutation of DP properties have received significant interest in recent years. In this work, we present a novel method for automated formal refutation of $ε$-DP. Our method refutes $ε$-DP by searching for a pair of inputs together with a non-negative function over outputs whose expected value on these two inputs differs by a significant amount. The two inputs and the non-negative function over outputs are computed simultaneously, by utilizing upper expectation supermartingales and lower expectation submartingales from probabilistic program analysis, which we leverage to introduce a sound and complete proof rule for $ε$-DP refutation. To the best of our knowledge, our method is the first method for $ε$-DP refutation to offer the following four desirable features: (1)~it is fully automated, (2)~it is applicable to stochastic mechanisms with sampling instructions from both discrete and continuous distributions, (3)~it provides soundness guarantees, and (4)~it provides semi-completeness guarantees. Our experiments show that our prototype tool SuperDP achieves superior performance compared to the state of the art and manages to refute $ε$-DP for a number of challenging examples collected from the literature, including ones that were out of the reach of prior methods.

</details>

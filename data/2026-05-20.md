### [1] [Rewrite System Showdown: Stochastic Search vs. EqSat](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19005)
*Qiantan Hong,Rupanshu Soi,Yihong Zhang,Alex Aiken*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文通过五个基准测试，将等式饱和（equality saturation）与随机搜索（stochastic search）进行系统比较，以回答“e-graphs是否真的有效”这一核心问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 等式饱和已成为等式程序优化的主流范式，但其从未与另一种可行方法（最显著的是随机搜索）进行过严格的比较，因此缺乏对其实际有效性的实证评估。

Method: 在五个基准测试上，将等式饱和方法与随机搜索方法进行对比实验，通过量化指标评估两者性能。

Result: 本文通过实验给出了等式饱和与随机搜索在五个基准上的比较结果。

Conclusion: 本文首次系统比较了等式饱和与随机搜索，为评估e-graphs的实际有效性提供了实证依据。

Abstract: Equality saturation has become a dominant paradigm for equational program optimization. However, it has never been rigorously compared to another approach to the same problem, even though several exist, the most notable being stochastic search. In this paper, we compare equality saturation to stochastic search over five benchmarks to answer the question: are e-graphs actually good?

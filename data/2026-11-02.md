### [1] [Iris on Indirection Theory: Lightweight Higher-order Ghost State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41202)
*Darius Foo,Viet-Anh Nguyen,Wei-Ngan Chin*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该论文证明 Indirection Theory 可以达到与 Iris 同等的表达力：在 Lean 中给出 Indirection Theory 的轻量级形式化，将其泛化为 knot 中含有来自 Iris 风格资源代数函子的资源，并在此之上重建 Iris 基础逻辑，从而在无需域理论的前提下获得对并发与高阶状态语义模型的完整支持。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 构建并发与高阶状态的语义模型目前有两条路线：Iris 通过资源上的步进索引谓词与高阶幽灵状态提供完全一般的模型，但依赖大量难以学习、难以移植到其他证明助手的机械化域理论；Indirection Theory 借助普通依赖类型实现，更为简单，但由于其基础是分离代数，资源模型的表达力弱于 Iris 风格的高阶幽灵状态。作者希望弥合这一矛盾，兼得两者的简洁元理论与强表达力。

Method: 在 Lean 中给出 Indirection Theory 的轻量级机械化实现，并将其推广为 knot 中含有一个 Iris 风格资源代数函子提供的资源；在此基础上重建 Iris 基础逻辑。整个构造刻意保持在初等元理论层面，不使用域理论。

Result: 实现了在 Lean 中的形式化构造，并通过两个案例研究表明所得基础逻辑既具表达力又忠实于 Iris：一是针对高阶状态的双正交逻辑关系（biorthogonal logical relation），二是包含不变量与用户可定义幽灵状态的 Iris 风格程序逻辑，其完备程度足以验证并发与高阶程序。

Conclusion: Indirection Theory 经推广后可具备与 Iris 相当的表达力，同时保留依赖类型带来的初等元理论优势；用户可跟随整段构造而不需域理论知识，为在更多证明助手中构建并发高阶语义模型提供了更易掌握与移植的路径。

Abstract: Today, semantic models of languages with concurrency and higher-order state are constructed using step-indexed predicates over resources and higher-order ghost state. There are two main lines of work for constructing such models. On the one hand, Iris provides these in full generality, at the cost of a large body of mechanised domain theory that is difficult to learn and port to new proof assistants. On the other hand, Indirection Theory is much simpler through the use of ordinary dependent types, but its foundation in separation algebra limits the expressiveness of its resource model compared to Iris-style higher-order ghost state. We resolve this conundrum by showing that Indirection Theory can be as expressive as Iris. To that end, we give a lightweight mechanisation of Indirection Theory in Lean, generalised so knots contain resources from an Iris-style resource algebra functor. We then reconstruct the Iris base logic on top of it. The advantage of this approach is in the elementary metatheory: a user can follow the whole construction without domain theory. We conduct two case studies to show that the base logic is expressive and faithful to Iris: a biorthogonal logical relation for higher-order state, and an Iris-style program logic with invariants and user-definable ghost state, complete enough to verify concurrent and higher-order programs.

### [1] [Model Checking Probabilistic Multiparty Session Types](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14795)
*Aleksander Junge,Leo Seojun Lee,Joseph Paulus,Nobuko Yoshida,David Parker*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出首个通用多参与者会话类型（MPST）的概率扩展，自底向上为会话定型，并由此推导会话的定量性质，包含概率/非确定性分离选择、概率子类型与合并算子、可靠且完备的PRISM翻译。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 分布式系统日益依赖随机化，但针对这类系统的验证技术与构建它们所用的编程抽象相互割裂。已有概率MPST系统无法同时支持"分离选择"（选择/分支的不同分支可由不同参与者发起）与非确定性抽象。

Method: 在不依赖全局规范的情况下自底向上为会话定型；类型中融合概率选择与抽象数据相关控制的非确定性选择，并允许分离选择。为此提出新的概率子类型关系与合并算子以证明类型可靠性（type soundness）与会话保真性（session fidelity）。定型上下文语义将非确定性消解、概率选择与同步三者分离，使每个定型上下文诱导一个有限马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）；在该MDP上推导会话无死锁与活性概率的可靠下界（对所有公平对抗调度成立）。此外给出首个从会话类型到PRISM建模语言的翻译，并证明其相对操作语义的可靠性与完备性。

Result: 得到在任意公平对抗调度下均成立的无死锁性与活性的概率下界——这是概率会话演算中首个（哪怕是部分性的）行为性质保证。翻译到PRISM被证明可靠且完备，工具链Prose实现了该框架，可自动检查安全性、无死锁性与活性。

Conclusion: 工作将概率MPST与会话演算的定量验证打通，验证技术与编程抽象得以统一，并为概率会话提供可机检（PRISM/Prose）的定量行为保证。

Abstract: Distributed systems increasingly rely on randomisation; yet, verification techniques for such systems remain separate from the programming abstractions used to build them. This paper proposes the first probabilistic extension of general multiparty session types - typing sessions bottom-up, with no global specification - deriving quantitative properties of a multiparty session from its session types alone. Our types combine probabilistic choice with nondeterministic choice that abstracts data-dependent control, and allow separated choices, where each branch of a selection or branching may involve a different participant - a combination beyond previous probabilistic multiparty session type systems. To achieve this, we develop a novel probabilistic subtyping relation and merge operator, using which we prove type soundness and session fidelity. The semantics of typing contexts separates nondeterministic resolution, probabilistic choice, and synchronisation, so that every typing context induces a finite Markov decision process. On it, we derive sound lower bounds, holding under every fair adversarial scheduler, on the probability that a session is deadlock-free and live - the first behavioural property guarantees, partial or otherwise, for a probabilistic session calculus. To connect session types with probabilistic model checking, we introduce the first translation from session types into the PRISM modelling language, and prove it sound and complete with respect to our operational semantics. We implement the framework in the toolchain Prose, which translates probabilistic multiparty session typing contexts into PRISM and checks safety, deadlock-freedom, and liveness.

### [2] [MLIRBench - A Benchmark and Dataset for Compiler and Hardware-Aware Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14881)
*Pranava Sai Maganti,Meghasyam Peddireddy,Muzakker Hossain,Kaining Gao,Farhan Ahmed,Ali Jannesari*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: MLIRBench：利用执行验证的程序对，评测大语言模型对MLIR编译器中间表示的语义推理能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM在软件工程任务上表现良好，但难以判断其真正理解程序行为还是仅依赖训练模式；中间表示（IR）显式刻画运算、数据流与执行语义，是检验该问题的理想载体，而现有缺乏针对MLIR的推理评测基准。

Method: 提出MLIRBench基准：采用多智能体流水线生成程序变异，通过实际执行验证其行为，再由torch与linalg程序对构建推理任务；数据含1,768个经验证的程序对（888个linalg对、880个torch对），标注为EQUIV、DIVERGE或ERROR三类。评测9个模型（5个开源权重、4个闭源）。

Result: Gemini 3.1 Pro在全部1,768对上取得最高硬标签准确率92.65%（预测失败计为错误）；其1,768次评分预测的聚合得分为74.18，评审给出的结果行为得分为5.04/10。

Conclusion: 强语义分类能力并不必然意味着能准确预测细粒度执行行为，二者间存在差距，揭示当前LLM在编译器IR上的推理短板；MLIRBench以执行验证的程序对为基础，为研究IR语义推理及程序修复、语义分类等任务提供可复现基础。

Abstract: In recent years, large language models (LLMs) have become increasingly popular in software engineering and have performed well on different programming tasks. However, it is still difficult to know whether these models truly understand program behavior or whether they mainly rely on patterns learned from their training data. This distinction is particularly important for intermediate representations (IRs), where operations, dataflow, and execution semantics are explicitly represented. In this paper, we introduce MLIRBench, a benchmark for evaluating how LLMs reason about MLIR programs. MLIRBench uses a multi-agent pipeline to generate program mutations, validate their behavior through execution, and construct reasoning tasks from torch and linalg program pairs. The dataset contains 1,768 validated pairs, including 888 linalg pairs and 880 torch pairs, labeled as EQUIV, DIVERGE, or ERROR. We evaluated nine models, including five open-weight and four proprietary models. Gemini 3.1 Pro achieved the highest hard-label accuracy of 92.65% across all 1,768 pairs, counting unsuccessful predictions as incorrect. Across its 1,768 scored predictions, it achieved an aggregate score of 74.18 and a judge-assigned result-behavior score of 5.04/10. These results show that strong semantic classification does not necessarily imply accurate prediction of detailed execution behavior, highlighting a gap in current LLM reasoning over compiler IRs. By exposing this gap through execution-validated program pairs, MLIRBench provides a reproducible foundation for studying semantic reasoning over compiler IRs and related tasks such as program repair and semantic classification.

### [3] [Mathematical foundations for symmetric programming and reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17106)
*Ohad Kammar,Matija Pretnar*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 论文提出一种基于"不失一般性"(w.l.o.g.)证明原则的数学抽象，利用群作用与canoniser将定义在规范输入上的函数唯一地、等变地延拓到整个定义域，并推广到依赖类型与代数数据类型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 编程与推理中普遍存在对称性，但缺乏统一、可复用的机制来系统地利用这些对称性（如传统w.l.o.g.论证多为手工、特例化处理）。论文希望给出一个统一框架来刻画并利用对称性。

Method: 给定描述输入与输出对称性的群作用，以及刻画任意输入如何被映射到规范形式的canoniser，构造将任意从规范输入出发的函数延拓到整个定义域；刻画使该延拓为唯一等变延拓的canoniser条件；通过Curry-Howard对应将构造适配到依赖类型设定；并推广到代数数据类型，由构造子的对称性唯一确定数据类型的对称性以支持高效执行。

Result: 给出该构造产生唯一等变延拓的canoniser充要/充分条件；在依赖类型框架下复现传统对称性w.l.o.g.论证；在代数数据类型上由构造子对称性唯一确定数据类型对称性，并为高效执行提供基础。

Conclusion: 该工作为在编程与形式推理中统一利用对称性提供了构造性数学基础，将w.l.o.g.原则形式化，并具备向依赖类型与代数数据类型推广的可落地性。

Abstract: We develop a mathematical abstraction for uniformly exploiting symmetries in programming and reasoning based on the mathematical proof principle `without-loss-of-generality' (w.l.o.g.). Given: group actions that describe symmetries of the input and the output; and a canoniser, which captures how arbitrary inputs get sent to canonical ones, our construction extends any function from the canonical inputs to the whole domain.
  We characterise the conditions on the canonisers which guarantee that the construction gives the unique equivariant (i.e. symmetry-preserving) extension. Furthermore, we adapt the construction to a dependently-typed setting, where it captures traditional symmetric w.l.o.g. arguments via the Curry-Howard correspondence. Finally, we extend the construction to algebraic data types, where the symmetries of the constructors both uniquely determine the symmetries of the data type and pave the way for efficient execution.

</details>

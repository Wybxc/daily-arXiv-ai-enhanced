### [1] [Parametrizing Reads-From Equivalence for Predictive Monitoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06533)
*Azadeh Farzan,Umang Mathur*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出k-切片重排序作为并发程序预测运行时监控的参数化框架，在表达能力和计算成本之间提供系统性的权衡。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 预测运行时监控需要在表达能力和计算复杂度之间取得平衡：读取等价性重排序表达能力最强但计算不可行，交换性重排序计算高效但表达能力有限。需要一种参数化方法来系统性地权衡这两个因素。

Method: 引入切片重排序及其推广形式k-切片重排序。σ的k-切片重排序ρ可以通过将σ划分为k+1个有序子序列并连接得到，同时保持程序顺序和读取约束。

Result: 1) k-切片重排序形成严格递增的层次结构，当k增长时收敛到读取等价性；2) 对于任意固定的k，针对任何正则规范的预测监控模k-切片重排序都允许常数空间流算法。

Conclusion: k-切片重排序为预测运行时监控提供了原则性的参数化框架，能够系统性地在表达能力和计算成本之间进行权衡，是现有等价关系的有效替代方案。

Abstract: Predictive runtime monitoring asks whether an execution $σ$ of a concurrent program can be used to \emph{soundly predict} the existence of a reordering $ρ$ of $σ$ that satisfies a property $\varphi$.
  Its effectiveness and efficiency depend on two factors: (a) the complexity of $\varphi$, and (b) the expressive power of the reorderings considered. At one extreme, allowing all reorderings induced by \emph{reads-from equivalence} makes predictive monitoring intractable, even for simple properties such as data races. At the other extreme, restricting to commutativity-based reorderings (Mazurkiewicz trace equivalence) yields efficient algorithms for simple properties, but remains intractable for general regular specifications and offers limited predictive power.
  We address this tradeoff via \emph{parametrization}. We introduce \emph{sliced reorderings} and their generalization, \emph{$k$-sliced reorderings}. Informally, $ρ$ is a $k$-sliced reordering of $σ$ if $σ$ can be partitioned into $k+1$ ordered subsequences whose concatenation yields $ρ$, while preserving program order and reads-from constraints.
  Our results are twofold. First, $k$-sliced reorderings form a strictly increasing hierarchy that converges to reads-from equivalence as $k$ grows. Second, for any fixed $k$, predictive monitoring modulo $k$-sliced reorderings against any regular specification admits a constant-space streaming algorithm.
  Together, these results establish $k$-sliced reorderings as a principled alternative to existing equivalences, enabling a uniform parametrized framework where expressive power can be systematically traded off against computational cost.

### [2] [Branching Out: Existential External Choice in Effpi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06875)
*Benjamin Robinson,Nobuko Yoshida*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: Effpi框架扩展：在Scala强类型消息传递系统中引入分支操作、多通道接收和超时处理，增强协议表达能力


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有Effpi工具包在表达能力上存在限制，需要增强协议表达能力以支持更复杂的消息传递模式，特别是需要支持外部选择、多通道消息接收和超时处理机制

Method: 1. 引入分支操作支持外部选择，根据接收消息的标签类型强制执行相应的协议延续；2. 扩展分支操作使其能够在多个通道上接收消息；3. 引入"catch timeout"操作，允许进程优雅处理无消息到达的情况；4. 通过编译器插件将行为类型编码为CCS变体来验证协议属性

Result: 增强了Effpi的表达能力，能够实现更复杂的消息传递协议，包括Raft共识算法的完整实现，同时保持了强类型验证和协议属性检查能力

Conclusion: 通过引入分支操作、多通道接收和超时处理机制，显著提升了Effpi框架的表达能力，使其能够支持更复杂的分布式系统协议，同时保持了类型安全和协议验证能力

Abstract: Effpi is a framework for writing strongly-typed message-passing programs in Scala, where the compiler enforces the conformance of process implementations to specified protocol types. A compiler plugin is provided to verify properties of protocols, such as deadlock-freedom and liveness, by encoding the behavioural types into a variant of CCS.
  To address limitations in the expressiveness of the existing toolkit, we extend Effpi with external choice by introducing a branching operation. Upon accepting a message via a branch, protocols enforce a continuation which depends on the label (type) of the received message. We equip the branching operation with the ability to accept messages over more than one channel. Additionally, we introduce a "catch timeout" operation to allow processes to gracefully handle a lack of incoming messages. The enhanced expressiveness of Effpi is demonstrated through a number of examples, including an implementation of the Raft consensus algorithm.

### [3] [Towards Multiparty Session Types for Highly-Concurrent and Fault-Tolerant Web Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06878)
*Richard Casetta,Nils Gesbert,Pierre Genevès*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种增强的多方会话类型（MPST）框架，为Web应用中的通信提供显式故障语义和动态参与机制，以处理状态更新、并发交互和外部服务不可靠性带来的挑战。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代Web应用结合了持久状态更新、并发交互和与外部服务的不可靠通信。超时等故障可能在部分状态更改后发生，产生临时不一致性，其解决依赖于实践中通常未验证的活性属性。虽然形式化方法为复杂软件推理提供严格保证，但由于其感知复杂性和缺乏实际自动化，在企业环境中很少采用。多方会话类型（MPST）为通信安全提供强大保证，但未考虑状态演化、动态工作流结构和故障行为之间的相互作用，而这些对于推理真实Web应用的正确性至关重要。

Method: 引入一个全局类型框架，为MPST配备显式故障语义和动态参与机制。定义了这些增强全局类型的语法和操作语义，并建立了核心属性，包括一致性保持。该基础支持在可能发生故障的Web应用中形式化推理通信，并为未来的状态扩展和活性属性自动化验证奠定基础。

Result: 提出了一个形式化框架，能够处理Web应用中通信的故障语义和动态参与。建立了增强全局类型的语法和语义，证明了包括一致性保持在内的核心属性。该框架为在故障可能发生的环境中形式化推理通信提供了基础。

Conclusion: 该论文通过为MPST引入显式故障语义和动态参与，解决了现代Web应用中状态演化、并发交互和不可靠通信的挑战。提出的框架为形式化推理故障环境下的通信提供了基础，并为未来状态扩展和活性属性自动化验证铺平了道路，有助于缩小形式化方法与企业实践之间的差距。

Abstract: Modern web applications combine persistent state updates, concurrent interactions, and unreliable communication with external services. Failures such as timeouts can occur after partial state changes, producing temporary inconsistencies whose resolution depends on liveness properties that are often not verified in practice. Although formal methods offer rigorous guarantees for reasoning about complex software, they remain rarely adopted in enterprise settings due to their perceived complexity and lack of practical automation. Multiparty Session Types (MPST) offer strong guarantees for communication safety, yet they do not account for the interplay between state evolution, dynamic workflow structure, and failure behaviour that are essential for reasoning about the correctness of real web applications.
  This paper introduces a global-type framework that equips MPST with explicit failure semantics and dynamic participation. We define the syntax and operational semantics of these enriched global types and establish core properties, including coherence preservation. This foundation enables formal reasoning about communications in web applications where failures may occur, and lays the groundwork for future stateful extensions and automated verification of liveness properties.

### [4] [Determinacy with Priorities up to Clocks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06879)
*Luigi Liquori,Michael Mendler,Claude Stolze*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 扩展CCS以支持优先级保护和时钟，引入相干性概念，用于组合式编码同步编程语言如Esterel


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Milner的CCS非确定性进程代数中，确定性/汇合性理论无法表达因果关系和共享内存多线程，且无法组合式编码Esterel等同步编程语言

Method: 扩展CCS，增加优先级保护动作和时钟机制，基于此扩展Milner的汇合性概念，提出新的相干性概念

Result: 提出的扩展CCS能够组合式编码Esterel等同步编程语言，解决了因果关系和共享内存多线程的表达问题

Conclusion: 通过优先级保护和时钟扩展CCS，并引入相干性概念，成功建立了同步编程语言与进程代数之间的组合式编码桥梁

Abstract: In Milner's seminal book on communication and concurrency introducing CCS, a process algebra inherently non-deterministic, chapter 11 was completely devoted to introduce the notion of determinacy and confluence in order to identify a subcalculus of CCS in which all definable agents are confluent. At the same time, or shortly later, determinate semantics were given for programming languages that reconcile concurrency and determinacy, such as Esterel by Berry and Gonthier, or SL by Boussinot and de Simone. These dedicated semantics do not easily map to Milner's confluence theory for CCS, which is unable to express causality and shared memory multi-threading with reaction to absence in a compositional way. We present an extension of CCS with priority-guarded actions and clocks, and we exploit the added expressiveness to enrich Milner's original notion of confluence by the new concept of coherence which permits us to encode, in a compositional fashion, synchronous programming languages such as Esterel.

</details>

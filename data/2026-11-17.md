### [1] [User-Schedulable Approximations for Quantized Tensor Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17526)
*Alex Reinking*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该论文将 Halide 的“算法与调度分离”扩展到数值表示层面，提出以 codec（编码器+解码器）描述量化近似，通过新指令 approximate_by 将 codec 应用于程序某一 stage，并由单一算法与调度库自动生成跨 Arm CPU 与 Apple GPU 的量化张量核，显著减少手工代码量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量化使语言模型可部署于边缘设备，但每种权重格式需与每种激活格式、每种指令集/GPU 组合配对一个 kernel（编码器与解码器须字节一致），维护成本剧增。在 GGML（llama.cpp 所用张量库）中这些 kernel 均手写，且在 Arm CPU 上运行的 54 个权度-激活对中仅 8 个具备分块矩阵乘 kernel。

Method: 把每个近似抽象为 codec（编码器与解码器配对），由可复用单元组合：quantizer、整数表示、bit packer 与 layout。引入近似指令 approximate_by，将一个 codec 作用于程序的一个 stage，并把编码器/解码器作为普通 stage 返回，交由 Halide 调度，从而使两个解码器可融合进矩阵乘的寄存器 tile。可选契约（如 round-trip 律）通过 property test 校验。

Result: 一套算法与一个调度库在 Arm CPU 与 Apple GPU 上为 GGML 和 KleidiAI 的 31 种格式生成 347 个 kernel，覆盖 38 个 GGML 尚无 kernel 的权度-激活对，代码量比 GGML+KleidiAI 少 3.7 倍。Apple M3 Max 上，91.0% 的对比情形达 GGML 专用 kernel 至少 95% 速度，中位加速 1.13x，在 GGML 回退到 blocked dot product 处更快；计入 Apple BLAS 时，CPU 情形仍有 86.9% 达到该阈值。移植到 x86（Intel AMX）仅增加 793 行代码，83.5% 情形与 GGML 持平。round-trip 契约检测出 GGML q8_1 中不一致的 block sum。

Conclusion: 将量化表示的编码/解码作为可调度的一等抽象，可实现一套算法+调度库跨多格式、多硬件自动生成量化 kernel，在性能上接近甚至超过手写专用实现，并大幅降低维护代码量；契约式属性测试还能发现既有库中的表示错误。

Abstract: Quantization lets language models run on edge devices, but it sharply increases the number of tensor kernels a library must maintain: every weight format must be paired with every activation format on every instruction set and GPU, with an encoder and a decoder that agree on the stored bytes. In GGML, the tensor library used by llama.cpp, these kernels are written by hand, and only 8 of the 54 weight-activation pairs it runs on our Arm CPU have a tiled matrix-multiplication kernel.
  We extend Halide's separation of algorithm and schedule to the representation of values. Each approximation is a codec, an encoder paired with a decoder, composed from reusable units: quantizers, integer representations, bit packers and layouts. A new directive, approximate_by, applies a codec to one stage of a program and returns the encoder and decoder as ordinary stages that Halide can schedule, so both decoders fuse into the register tiles of a matrix product. Optional contracts, such as a round-trip law, are checked by property tests; the law exposed an inconsistent block sum in GGML's q8_1.
  One algorithm and one schedule library generate 347 kernels for 31 of GGML's and KleidiAI's formats on Arm CPUs and Apple GPUs, covering 38 weight-activation pairs for which GGML has no kernel, in 3.7x less code than GGML and KleidiAI. On an Apple M3 Max, our kernels reach at least 95% of the speed of GGML's format-specific kernels in 91.0% of the cases that have one, with a median speedup of 1.13x, and are faster still where GGML falls back to blocked dot products. Counting Apple's BLAS library, which is usually faster for larger f32 products, we still reach that threshold in 86.9% of CPU cases. A port to x86 with Intel's AMX adds 793 lines of code and matches GGML in 83.5% of its cases.

*Eric Bond,Max New*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出一种统一构造：从刻画效应的代数理论同时导出指称语义与操作语义，在带代数理论操作的call-by-push-value上证明等式典范性，并给出基于树的及基于配置的小步语义；在affinity条件下保证终止并在ground type上计算指称值。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 带效应的语言通常有两条独立指定的语义：基于（自由）模型的指称语义，以及由效应特定归约规则定义的操作语义。这种分离导致两个独立构造的语义结构，其关系只能在事后（a posteriori）建立。作者希望消解这种二元性，从同一代数理论同时生成两者。

Method: 在扩展了代数理论操作的call-by-push-value（CBPV）上工作；证明等式典范性（equational canonicity）；给出基于树的操尔语义，将所有闭computation归约为操作树；当自由模型monad是monadic container时，从中导出基于配置（配置即自由模型元素）的小步语义，并证明每步保持指称；引入"affinity"条件以保证终止与ground type上的正确性；以state、errors、writer、加权与pre-convex概率选择作为实例。

Result: 对errors、writer与加权选择，恢复了熟悉的归约规则；对state，得到一个同时从所有初始状态运行程序的机器；在affinity条件下归约必终止，并在ground type上计算所得值等于指称。

Conclusion: 指称解释与操作归约可由同一代数理论统一构造，二者关系内蕴而非事后建立；该框架覆盖多种常见效应，并在适度条件下提供终止性与正确性保证。

Abstract: Languages with effects are typically given two independently specified semantic accounts: a denotational semantics based on (free) models, and an operational semantics defined by effect-specific reduction rules. This separation leads to two independently constructed semantic structures whose relationship must then be established a posteriori. We present a unified construction in which both denotational interpretation and operational reduction are constructed from an algebraic theory specifying the effects.
  We work with call-by-push-value extended by the operations of an algebraic theory. We prove equational canonicity and give a tree-based operational semantics that reduces every closed computation to a tree of operations. When the free model monad is a monadic container, we derive from it a configuration-based small-step semantics. Configurations are elements of the free model. Every step preserves the denotation. Under a condition we call affinity, every reduction terminates and at ground types computes the denotation. We instantiate the framework with state, errors, writer, and weighted and pre-convex probabilistic choice. For errors, writer and weighted choice we recover the familiar rules. For state we get a machine that runs the program from every initial state at once.

Motivation: DPOR中更粗的等价关系理论上可减少状态类数，但在具体程序上未必带来实际加速；需要一种针对单个程序量化粗等价优势的方法。

Method: 证明RF类计数与RF/Mazurkiewicz比值计算在顺序一致性下的#P-hard性及近似困难性；据此设计基于采样的CoEst算法：固定线程数时单次试验多项式时间，多次独立试验得到无偏估计且几乎必然收敛。

Result: 理论结果：两个计数问题均#P-hard，比值在标准复杂性假设下难以精确近似。实验：34个基准配置上，一分钟估计预算下以99.4%的比例选出更快的模型检测器。

Conclusion: 精确计算粗等价收益不可行，但采样估计可提供统计证据，实用地判断Mazurkiewicz与RF模型检测何者更快。

Abstract: Dynamic partial-order reduction (DPOR) has emerged as a promising approach to addressing interleaving explosion in bounded model checking of concurrent programs. Given an equivalence relation on interleavings, the model checker explores a program's executions while minimizing repeated visits to the same class; optimal model checkers avoid such repetition altogether. A coarser equivalence promises faster exploration by reducing the number of classes. This has motivated alternatives to Mazurkiewicz's trace equivalence such as reads-from and reads-value-from equivalence. This promise, however, does not always materialize in practice: while a coarser equivalence can yield exponentially fewer classes on some families of programs, it may offer little or no reduction for the program at hand.
  We show how to quantify the advantage of coarser equivalences for individual programs. We focus on the problem of counting the reads-from (RF) equivalence classes induced by a given program, as well as the problem of computing the ratio of RF to Mazurkiewicz class counts under sequential consistency. We show that both problems are $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard and that the reduction ratio remains hard to approximate accurately under standard complexity assumptions.
  Guided by these limits, we present CoEst, a sampling-based algorithm for estimating both the RF count and its ratio to the Mazurkiewicz count. For a fixed number of threads, each trial runs in polynomial time and yields unbiased count estimates. As the number of independent trials grows, the count and ratio estimates converge almost surely to their true values. Our algorithm can provide statistical evidence for deciding whether Mazurkiewicz or RF model checking is likely to be faster for a given program. In experiments across 34 benchmark configurations, our method selects the faster checker in 99.4% of runs using a one-minute estimation budget.

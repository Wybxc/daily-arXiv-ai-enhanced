### [1] [MetaphIR: Corpus-Scale Applicability Analysis for LLVM Peephole Optimizations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23555)
*Hongxu Xu,Xintong Zhou,Chunhao Liao,Chengnian Sun*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: MetaphIR 结合 DSL 与索引式搜索引擎，在大规模 LLVM IR 语料上以交互速度精确统计窥孔优化的适用函数数，20 项 InstCombine 优化上达到 100% 精确率与召回率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 编译器开发者在实现新的窥孔优化前，需要知道真实程序中源模式匹配且前置条件满足的频率。现有流程不足：对 480 万函数 IR 语料做结构搜索会忽略前置条件导致高估；而实现重写并重新编译语料虽精确，但工程成本高，按需重编译流水线中位耗时 47 分钟，在泛化重写、迭代多版本时尤其受限。

Method: MetaphIR 由两部分组成：一是受 LLVM C++ matcher 语法启发的 DSL，模式在 LLVM IR 上引入"洞"与对匹配值的布尔前置条件；二是面向大规模 IR 语料的索引式搜索引擎，预先构建紧凑的逐函数摘要（per-function summaries），并在摘要上做抽象解释，可靠地过滤掉不可能满足查询约束的函数。

Result: 在 LLVM InstCombine 近期 20 个窥孔优化上，MetaphIR 精确复原了各重写单独适用时的函数集合，精确率与召回率均为 100%；查询中位耗时 1.27 秒，相对全语料扫描提速 22 倍。

Conclusion: MetaphIR 使开发者能在实现优化前快速评估其覆盖面与泛化性，兼顾精确性与交互式响应速度，弥补了忽略前置条件的结构搜索与高成本重编译之间的空白。

Abstract: Peephole optimizations replace short source instruction sequences with more efficient equivalents, typically guarded by preconditions such as requiring a constant operand to be a power of two. Since every new rewrite adds maintenance burden and compile-time cost, compiler developers need to know how often real-world programs both match the source pattern and satisfy its preconditions before committing to an implementation. Existing workflows fall short: structural search over an intermediate representation (IR) corpus of 4.8 million functions ignores preconditions and therefore overcounts, whereas implementing the rewrite and recompiling the corpus yields exact counts only after paying the engineering cost, with each run of the corpus's on-demand recompilation pipeline taking a median of 47 minutes. This delay is especially limiting when generalizing a rewrite, as developers often iterate over multiple variants to trade off coverage against complexity.
  We introduce MetaphIR, a system pairing a domain-specific language for expressing LLVM peephole optimizations with an indexed search engine over large IR corpora. Using a syntax modeled on LLVM's C++ matchers, MetaphIR patterns augment LLVM IR with holes and Boolean preconditions over matched values. To answer queries at interactive speed, MetaphIR builds compact per-function summaries ahead of time and uses abstract interpretation over those summaries to soundly filter out functions that cannot satisfy a query's constraints. Across twenty recent peephole optimizations from LLVM's InstCombine pass, MetaphIR recovers the exact set of functions where each rewrite applies in isolation, achieving 100% precision and recall. Queries complete in a median of 1.27 seconds---a 22x speedup over a full corpus scan---enabling developers to quickly gauge an optimization's reach and generality prior to implementation.

### [2] [Detecting Empty Abstractions and Pruning Dead Paths in the Linux eBPF Verifier with Abstract Step Functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23584)
*Harishankar Vishwanatha,Srinivas Narayana,Santosh Nagarakatte*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出基于抽象步进函数（tnum_step）的常量时空集检测方法，用于eBPF验证器在多域分支精化时探测域间矛盾的死亡路径并即时剪枝。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: eBPF验证器用多个区间域与位域（tristate/tnum）跟踪寄存器状态，但缺乏空集（⊥）表示，无法识别多域交集为空的状态，导致对不可达路径继续分析；将矛盾状态传入域归约或下游算子违背非空前置条件，引发域不变量违规，长期造成生产环境宕机、误拒与状态爆炸。

Method: 提出抽象步进函数框架：在O(1)时间内判定区间域与非凸域（tnum）是否相交，无需逐值扫描；据此设计形式化验证的常量时间算子tnum_step，可直接跳到满足三态位掩码的下一个整数；再基于该算子构造多域空集检测，在分支精化阶段识别矛盾约束并立即剪枝死分支。

Result: 消除了死分支不变量违规的根因，抑制状态爆炸与误拒；在Linux内核自测及Cilium等生产套件上开销可忽略（1.48%）；tnum_step算子已合入Linux内核主线。

Conclusion: 在eBPF验证器抽象域间实现了可靠且完备的常量时空集检测，为死路径剪枝提供了通用、可形式化验证且可工程落地的方案。

Abstract: The Linux kernel's eBPF verifier uses abstract interpretation to ensure the safety of user-provided programs before execution. It tracks each register with multiple interval domains and a bitwise domain (tristate numbers). Refining the abstract state at conditional branches can produce mutually contradictory constraints across domains, indicating an unreachable path. However, the verifier lacks an empty-set ($\bot$) representation and cannot detect when these domains have an empty intersection, causing it to analyze dead paths. Passing such contradictory states into domain reduction or downstream operators violates their implicit non-emptiness precondition, triggering domain invariant violations. This has been a multi-year issue in the Linux kernel causing production outages, false rejections, and state explosion.
  This paper presents a sound and complete constant-time emptiness check across the eBPF verifier's abstract domains to prune dead branches. We introduce abstract step functions, a general framework that tests whether intervals and non-convex domains intersect in O(1) time without scanning individual values. We develop tnum_step, a formally verified constant-time operator that jumps to the next integer satisfying a tristate bitmask. Using tnum_step, we design a multi-domain emptiness check that detects contradictory states during branch refinement and immediately prunes unreachable branches, eliminating the root cause of dead-branch invariant violations. Our technique prevents invariant violations, state explosion, and false rejections while incurring negligible (1.48%) overhead across Linux kernel selftests and production suites such as Cilium. Our tnum_step operator has been upstreamed into the mainline Linux kernel.

</details>


### [3] [Validating Concurrent Correctness Witnesses with Ghosts by Thread-Modular Abstract Interpretation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23792)
*Michael Schwarz,Zhendong Ang,Umang Mathur*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出一种利用ghost变量语义信息验证并发正确性witness的新框架，将检查阶段与利用阶段统一为线程模块化抽象解释。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 并发程序验证难，且验证器本身可能因实现缺陷给出错误结论；正确性witness可支持独立验证以增强可信度。现有并发witness虽引入ghost变量编码更强不变量，但验证技术缺失：验证器未利用ghost中的语义信息指导验证，仅把ghost插桩与ghost不变量当作额外证明义务，导致无法验证超出无引导线程模块化分析能力的不变量。

Method: 框架分两阶段，均为线程模块化抽象解释：检查阶段确立ghost插桩的语义内容，利用阶段使用该信息。考虑两类ghost变量及对应利用技术：基于资源不变量（resource invariants）的一类，和基于阶段（phases）的一类。阶段ghost用于控制抽象解释考虑交错的程度，而非始终粗粒度地抽象交错。

Result: 在静态分析器中实现该框架，并在两类witness上评估：来自先进软件验证器生成的编码资源不变量的witness，以及手工构造的编码阶段信息的witness。

Conclusion: 利用ghost变量语义信息的验证框架可验证超出无引导线程模块化分析范围的不变量witness，同时保留线程模块化分析的可扩展性优势。

Abstract: Concurrent programs are notoriously difficult to write and to reason about, and even tools based on sound theoretical foundations can produce incorrect results due to implementation bugs. Correctness witnesses have been proposed as a way to increase the confidence in the verdicts of software verifiers by enabling independent validation. Recently, correctness witnesses have been extended to the concurrent setting, by introducing ghost variables to allow encoding more expressive invariants. While different approaches to producing witnesses with ghosts exist, validation techniques are still lacking; instead of leveraging the information encoded in the ghosts to guide the validation process, validators merely treat ghost instrumentation and invariants over ghost variables as additional proof obligations. We propose a novel framework for the validation of concurrent correctness witnesses that exploits the semantic information encoded in ghost variables. It combines a checking phase, which establishes the semantic content of the ghost instrumentation, with an exploitation phase making use of this information. Both phases are formulated as thread-modular abstract interpretation. In particular, we consider two different classes of ghost variables and techniques for exploiting them, one based on resource invariants and one based on phases. Phase ghosts guide the degree to which the abstract interpretation considers interleavings instead of always abstracting them coarsely. In this way, the abstract interpreter can validate witnesses containing invariants beyond the reach of unguided thread-modular analysis, while still retaining its scalability benefits. We have implemented our framework in a static analyzer and evaluate it on witnesses encoding resource invariants obtained from a state-of-the-art software verifier, as well as on hand-crafted witnesses encoding phase information.

</details>


### [4] [A Principled Approach to Concurrency, Nondeterminism, and Asynchrony in Choreographies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24649)
*Xueying Qin,Marco Peressotti,Fabrizio Montesi*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出 Nyarlathotep，首个同时支持异步与不确定性的编排编程（CP）框架；给出编排与网络的 LTS 操作语义，证明端点投影（EPP）的可靠性与完备性、通信安全与无死锁，并在 Lean 4 中机械化全部结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有 CP 理论缺乏将异步性（进程独立推进）与不确定性（交互产生的多种行为）统一整合的框架，二者尚未被同时纳入 CP 理论。

Method: 构建 Nyarlathotep 框架，用标记转移系统（LTS）分别定义编排与网络的操作语义；在异步与编排不确定性之外，支持一般分支、乱序执行、以及进程可独立进入的带参数过程的一般递归；证明 EPP 的可靠性与完备性，建立编排语义与投影网络语义之间的强行为等价，并证明投影网络通信安全、无死锁；全部结果在 Lean 4 中机械化验证。

Result: 获得 EPP 的可靠性与完备性，即编排与其投影网络之间的强行为等价；并证得投影网络通信安全且无死锁；所有定理均已在 Lean 4 中形式化验证。

Conclusion: Nyarlathotep 首次在同一 CP 理论中统一异步性与不确定性，并借助机器验证的元理论保证投影网络的正确性与安全性，为后续扩展 CP 框架提供了可信基础。

Abstract: Choreographic programming (CP) describes a distributed system as a global program and compiles it into communicating network programs via endpoint projection (EPP). Asynchrony captures the independent progress of communicating processes, while nondeterminism captures the range of behaviours that can arise from their interactions. However, the integration of both features into a theory of CP is absent.
  We present Nyarlathotep, the first asynchronous nondeterministic CP framework. We define its operational semantics of choreographies and networks using labelled transition systems. Besides asynchrony and choreographic nondeterminism, Nyarlathotep supports expressive features including general branching, out-of-order execution, and general recursion with parameterised procedures that processes can enter independently. We prove EPP sound and complete, establishing strong behavioural equivalence between the semantics of choreographies and their projected networks. We prove that projected networks are communication-safe and deadlock-free. We mechanised all our results in Lean 4.

</details>

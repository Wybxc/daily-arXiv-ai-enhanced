### [1] [Untrusted Authors, Trusted Answers: A Calculus of Fidelity-Graded Translations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14137)
*Christoph Kirsch*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出了一种基于保真度等级的翻译验证演算，将翻译视为多语言多目标图，通过可组合的交换方块和弱链等级聚合实现程序级验证，并在Lean 4中机械化了核心部分。该演算在hurdy-gurdy平台上实现，包含13种语言和13个翻译对，完全由LLM生成代码，人类贡献仅在于架构设计。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统验证翻译（认证编译、翻译验证）均将单次翻译孤立处理，忽视了多源语言、多推理目标、多条独立构建路径构成的翻译图场景。在这种图中，"这个翻译是否正确？"的答案因边而异，缺乏统一的可组合验证框架。

Method: 提出保真度分级翻译演算：程序级可检查的交换方块通过拼接组合；声明保真度等级通过弱链法则聚合，每次运行时通过内联检查重新建立，独立推导的路由之间的协议可超越声明等级；端到端定理揭示了根本不对称性——携带见证的答案在源端是自认证的，而全称答案才是等级、分支和证书发挥价值之处。核心部分在Lean 4中机械化实现。

Result: 在hurdy-gurdy平台上实现了13种语言和13个翻译对，围绕两个推理中心构建为双向实验；LLM代理在基本无监督下编写每个翻译对，以架构的交叉检查作为唯一语义门控。报告了覆盖范围、分支协议、合规性基准（含机器生成的地面真值）、见证重放、认证不可达性和门控逃逸率实验。

Conclusion: 该架构支持通过任何人（使用LLM、代理或手工）贡献的翻译对实现语言支持的扩展，准入依据是架构而非作者身份。核心贡献在于架构设计本身，而非代码实现。

Abstract: Verified translation has two well-studied extremes: prove the translator once (certified compilation), or validate each run of one translator (translation validation). Both treat a single translation in isolation. We study translations as a graph -- many source languages, several reasoning targets, multiple independently built routes -- where the honest answer to "is this translation correct?" differs from edge to edge. We present a calculus of fidelity-graded translations: pairs of languages close commuting squares that are checkable per program and compose by pasting; declared fidelity grades compose by weakest link, are re-established per run by inline checking, and are exceeded by agreement between independently derived routes; and an end-to-end theorem isolates a fundamental asymmetry -- witness-carrying answers are self-certifying at the source, while universal answers are where grades, branches, and certificates earn their cost. The compositional core is mechanized in Lean 4.
  The calculus is implemented in hurdy-gurdy, a platform of 13 languages and 13 pairs around two reasoning hubs, built as a two-directional experiment in LLM-generated correctness: independent LLM agents wrote every pair, largely unsupervised, with the architecture's cross-checks as the only semantic gate, and the platform's intended player is itself an LLM. All code, and most of this paper, is LLM-generated; the human contribution is the architecture. The same gate is the intended growth model: hurdy-gurdy scales in language support through pairs contributed by anyone -- with LLMs, with agents, or by hand -- admitted by architecture, not authorship. We report conjoined coverage, branch agreement, a compliance-derived benchmark with machine-derived ground truth, witness replay, certified unreachability, and escape-rate experiments for the gate.

### [2] [Volition Elicitation: Operational Semantics for People and Their Machines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14138)
*Ehud Shapiro*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了vGLP语言，将人的意愿（volition）作为多智能体原子事务的守卫条件，通过AI从事务规范生成程序并自动映射到用户界面，在实体手机上实现了社交图谱、社交网络和货币等去中心化应用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有分布式系统通常包含人和个人设备，计算由人的意愿驱动（如支付、交友）。需要一种形式化规范语言来建模这种"意愿守卫"的多智能体原子事务，并实现从抽象规范到可运行应用的自动化。

Method: 定义volition-guarded GLP (vGLP)语言，扩展GLP加入意愿守卫子句；将其操作语义定义为通信意愿智能体（Communicating Volitional Agents）的实例；通过AI从意愿守卫多智能体原子事务生成vGLP程序，再映射到UI构造。

Result: 在社交图谱、社交网络和货币三个去中心化平台上验证了该方法：每个平台都是由AI从规范生成的vGLP程序，最终部署在实体智能手机上成为可运行的应用。

Conclusion: 正确实现必须获取人的意愿（即"探知人脑中的想法"），这也是用户界面的唯一存在理由；vGLP的UI构造能自然映射意愿守卫子句，实现从规范到应用的全流程自动化。

Abstract: The most prevalent distributed systems today include people and their personal machines (smartphones). In such systems, computations are driven by people's volitions: a payment when a person wishes to pay someone, befriending when two people wish to become friends, etc. Volition-Guarded Multiagent Atomic Transactions were proposed as an abstract specification language for such systems, in which each agent consists of a person and their machine, and a transaction can be guarded by both the machine states and the personal volitions of its participating agents. Here, we define the programming language volition-guarded GLP (vGLP), which extends GLP with volition-guarded clauses, and define its operational semantics as an instance of Communicating Volitional Agents. As the semantics requires the person to will a volition-guarded clause reduction, a correct implementation must elicit the person's volitions: finding out ``what's in the person's head'' is the sole rationale for the UI, which is realised accordingly by standard constructs.
  We demonstrate the approach on the grassroots social graph, social network, and currencies: each platform is a vGLP program, generated by AI from volition-guarded multiagent atomic transactions; the implementation of vGLP, also created by AI, then maps its volition-guarded clauses into the user-interface constructs, resulting in a single working app deployed on a physical smartphone.

Abstract: Two-sided type systems, introduced in POPL'24, are an extension of the traditional notion of type system that allows for stating and deriving typing judgements in which (a) assumptions can be made about the types of arbitrary terms and not only variables, and (b) conclusions can be made about any number of type assignments, and not exactly one. We introduce new two-sided type systems 2$λ$Int and 2$λ$Int$^{\sim}$ that correspond with Wansing's bilateral logic 2Int and its extension with Nelson's strong negation respectively. Going beyond the propositional case, we introduce 2$λ$HOL as an extension of Guevers' $λ$HOL, and we show its expressive adequacy, its consistency and that it satisfies both the existence property and its dual.

### [4] [A Fast Quantitative Analyzer for NetKAT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14420)
*Thomas Lu,Qiancheng Fu,Kevin Batz,Oliver Bøving,Tiago Ferreira,Mark Moeller,Nate Foster,Alexandra Silva*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于加权NetKAT（wNetKAT）的快速量化网络性质分析器，通过设计符号数据结构wSPPs和迹携带帕累托半环，实现了高效的单一和多目标网络量化分析，在性能上显著优于现有工具。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Result: 该实现在参数化半环下既支持经典布尔可达性分析（与Katch竞争），又在概率分析上比McNetKAT和Storm快数个数量级；以Fat-tree与Jellyfish数据中心拓扑的案例研究展示了多目标设计时分析能力。

Conclusion: 该工作为网络量化性质分析提供了统一、高效的形式化框架，在单一目标和多目标场景下均显著优于现有工具，展示了实际工程应用的潜力。

Abstract: When designing a network, engineers must navigate trade-offs (e.g., one topology offers more aggregate bandwidth, another lower latency or better resilience) that demand reasoning about quantitative properties. We show how to compute all policy constructs symbolically; unsurprisingly, the crux is Kleene star, for which we design a tailored algorithm. We further develop trace-carrying Pareto semirings, which compute multi-objective frontiers together with the network paths that realize them. We formalize the development in Lean and provide an optimized Rust implementation. Being parametric on a semiring, our implementation covers both classical and quantitative analyses: we show that it is competitive with KATch, a heavily optimized Boolean-reachability verifier, and orders of magnitude faster than McNetKAT and Storm on probabilistic analyses. A case study comparing Fat-tree and Jellyfish data-center topologies shows the framework supports multi-objective design-time analysis.

### [5] [Simply Typed Reverse-Mode AD with Variants: Denotational Correctness via Idempotent Completion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14453)
*Fernando Lucatelli Nunes,Diogo Simm,Matthijs Vákár*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文证明了反向模式自动微分中，原本需要依赖类型表示的变体类型的余切空间，可以通过在普通非依赖类型目标中嵌入余切纤维并使用原始索引的幂等算子来选择有效纤维来实现。通过Karoubi完备化理论，建立了常值族模型与其完备化之间的等价关系，从而证明了依赖余切族和带有投影算子的简单类型余切空间是同一指称变换的等价表示。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 反向模式自动微分的指称语义中，变体类型（variant types）阻碍了简单的类型化表示，因为有效的余切空间取决于运行时选择的分支。现有的正确性结果使用依赖类型的原始索引余切空间族，但依赖类型实现复杂。作者希望在普通的非依赖类型目标中表示相同的依赖关系。

Method: 将每个源类型的余切纤维嵌入到共同的 ambient 类型中，使用原始索引的幂等算子选择有效纤维。从语义上，这相当于从常值族模型过渡到其Karoubi完备化。作者证明了对于范畴𝒞和正则无限基数κ，当𝒞是Cauchy完备且每个κ-小族都有公共收缩宿主时，常值族包含可扩展为等价关系Kar(Copow_κ(𝒞)) ≃ Fam_κ(𝒞)。

Result: 得到了一个使用普通目标类型、投影算子和反向传播算子的反向模式自动微分的双笛卡尔闭语义。分裂生成的幂等算子可以恢复已有的依赖语义。证明了依赖余切族和带有投影算子的简单类型余切空间是同一指称变换的等价表示。

Conclusion: 依赖类型的余切空间族与简单类型的带投影余切空间是等价的，为反向模式自动微分中变体类型的处理提供了更简洁的语义基础。

Abstract: Reverse-mode automatic differentiation is commonly given a denotational account in which each source type has a single cotangent type. Variant types obstruct this simply typed representation because the valid cotangent space depends on the branch selected at run time. Existing correctness results therefore use primal-indexed families of cotangent spaces, whose natural internal language is dependently typed.
  We show that the same dependency can be represented in an ordinary nondependent target. The cotangent fibres of each source type are embedded in a common ambient type, and a primal-indexed idempotent selects the valid fibre. Semantically, this amounts to passing from the constant-family model to its Karoubi completion.
  For a category $\mathcal C$ and a regular infinite cardinal $κ$, we prove that the constant-family inclusion extends to an equivalence $\mathrm{Kar}(\mathrm{Copow}*κ(\mathcal C)) \simeq \mathrm{Fam}*κ(\mathcal C)$ precisely when $\mathcal C$ is Cauchy complete and every $κ$-small family admits a common retract host. We also construct the resulting coproducts explicitly.
  Applying this theorem, we obtain a bicartesian closed semantics for reverse-mode automatic differentiation with variants using only ordinary target types, projectors, and backpropagators. Splitting the generated idempotents recovers the established dependent semantics. Thus dependent cotangent families and simply typed ambient cotangents equipped with projectors are equivalent presentations of the same denotational transformation.

### [6] [Mech: Mechanised Choreographic Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15174)
*Xueying Qin,Marco Peressotti,Fabrizio Montesi*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该论文提出了Mech，一个在Lean 4中实现的编舞编程机械化框架，支持知识选择通用分支、通用递归和非确定性选择等丰富特性，并证明了端点投影的完备性与正确性，以及通信安全和无死锁性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有编舞编程的机械化只处理受限片段，而具有丰富特性的通用语言实现中关键交互关系被非形式化地处理，特别是知识选择通用分支、通用递归和非确定性选择尚未集成到机器检查的理论中。

Method: 在Lean 4中实现Mech机械化框架，重新制定语义以正确捕获非确定性选择与并发的交互，并发现编舞的代数定律以简化证明。

Result: 证明了端点投影的完备性和正确性，并推导出投影网络的通信安全性和无死锁性质，形成了迄今为止最广泛的编舞编程机械化理论。

Conclusion: Mech成功地将编舞编程的核心特性集成到机器检查的理论中，解决了非确定性选择与并发交互的语义问题，并通过代数定律简化了复杂证明。

Abstract: Choreographic programming (CP) is a programming paradigm for the correct-by-construction development of concurrent and distributed systems: programmers write the intended overall behaviour of a system from a global perspective in a choreography, which is then automatically compiled into communicating endpoint programs by a procedure known as endpoint projection (EPP). The central promise is that the projected endpoint programs, when executed together, are behaviourally equivalent to the source choreography.
  Fulfilling this promise becomes delicate for expressive CP languages. Existing mechanisations of CP treat only restricted fragments, while textbook and general purpose language implementations with rich features leave crucial interactions informal. In particular, general branching in knowledge of choice, general recursion, and nondeterministic choice in choreographies have not yet been integrated in a machine-checked theory.
  We present Mech, a new mechanisation of CP in Lean 4 that captures these features. There are two central technical challenges in our development of Mech. First, the sketched semantics from the literature does not correctly capture how nondeterministic choice interacts with concurrency. We therefore formulate new semantics that align nondeterministic choreographic executions with the behaviours of projected endpoint programs. Second, managing all these features in proofs is complex. We address this by uncovering new algebraic laws for choreographies, the operators used in their semantics, EPP, and their combinations. Using our development, we prove completeness and soundness of EPP and derive communication safety and deadlock-freedom for projected networks, yielding the most extensive mechanised theory of CP to date.

### [1] [The Bell Experiment, Monadically](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26777)
*Chenchao Ding,Amr Sabry*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该文用 Haskell 将两方 Bell 实验写成程序，把 Bell 定理的三条独立性假设（blind、local、factored）映射为程序结构与单子（monad）组合方式，逐一放弃并运行以得到各类非经典箱体及其关联界限。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统 Bell 定理讨论停留在数学定理层面，作者希望把实验的"结构"、各方可见信息与组合方式显式化，用程序设计语言的形式化结构来承载并定位 Bell 定理所依赖的独立性假设，从而使各种假设的取舍与其物理后果（漏洞、超决定论、量子箱、PR 箱、信号）之间的关系变得可执行、可比对。

Method: 将两方 Bell 实验实现为一个小型 Haskell 程序：两方的箱子在一个 monad 中计算，调度并重复试验的实验在另一个 monad 中计算，用"membrane"把前者运行于后者之内。每次运行记录其试验并在单一表格（该运行的经验模型）上读出 CHSH 值与是否信号两个判据。把"经典模型"定义为三条性质：blind（隐策略抽取时看不到测量设置）、local（给定策略，任一方结果不依赖另一方设置）、factored（给定策略与设置，两结果独立）；然后逐一去除这些性质并运行所得模型。此外比较两种无信号检验方式：直接从运行自身的表格读出，与在固定设置下用独立实验估计。

Result: 三种性质同时成立给出经典界 2。放弃 blind 得到实验漏洞与超决定论；放弃 factoring 得到量子箱（达到 2√2）与假想的 PR 箱（达到 4），二者均无信号；放弃 locality 得到改写共享隐状态的箱体，其是否信号取决于该状态的采样方式。

Conclusion: Bell 定理的独立性假设可以在程序结构中被精确定位，并以 monad 分层与 membrane 组合的形式体现；逐条放宽假设对应可运行且界限明确的非经典模型族。无信号性可从单次运行的表格直接读出，也可通过固定设置下的独立实验估计，二者构成需要比较的两种检验路径。

Abstract: Bell's theorem says that no classical model satisfying certain independence assumptions can reproduce the correlations that quantum mechanics predicts and experiments observe. In this paper we write the two-party Bell experiment as a small Haskell program, and find those assumptions in its structure: in what each part of the program can see, and in how the parts are composed. The boxes of the two parties compute in one monad, and the experiment that schedules and repeats their trials computes in another; a \emph{membrane} runs the first inside the second. A run keeps the record of its trials, and both of its verdicts, the CHSH value and whether the boxes signal, are read off one table: the empirical model of that run.
  In this setting, a model is classical when it has three properties. It is \emph{blind}: the hidden strategy of the boxes is drawn without seeing the measurement settings. It is \emph{local}: given that strategy, neither box's outcome depends on the other party's setting. It is \emph{factored}: given the strategy and the settings, the two outcomes are independent. Together, they give the classical bound of $2$. We give them up one at a time and run what results. Without blindness, we get experimental loopholes and superdeterminism. Without factoring, we get quantum boxes, which reach $2\sqrt2$, and the hypothetical PR boxes, which reach $4$, both without signaling. Without locality, we get boxes that rewrite a shared hidden state; whether they signal depends on how that state is sampled.
  We also compare two ways to check no-signaling: reading it off the run's own table, and estimating it in separate experiments at fixed settings.

### [2] [Compositional Temporal Specifications for Interaction Trees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27553)
*Linyu Yang,Jianwei Yin,Mingshuai Chen*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该论文提出基于Choice Trees的浅嵌入时序规约语言iBuchi，用于对Interaction Trees（ITrees）进行组合式时序推理，并集成到Dijkstra Monads Forever框架中以支持与Hoare风格规约的组合，已在Rocq中形式化验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代effectful程序的正确性不仅取决于最终结果，还取决于与环境的可观察交互的时间演化。ITrees提供了可复用的指称语义，但对其可复用的时序推理仍基本未被探索。

Method: 引入iBuchi——一种基于Choice Trees的浅嵌入时序规约语言，表示有限与无限交互轨迹，支持顺序、分支、合取、重复以及线性时序逻辑（LTL）模态等表达性时序算子；定义轨迹级与树级的满足关系及组合式推理原则；将iBuchi集成进Dijkstra Monads Forever框架，使时序规约能与Hoare风格规约组合，并支持基于生成器的未来条件编码。

Result: 在Rocq中正式开发并验证了该框架，并通过多个非平凡案例研究展示了其表达力与组合性。

Conclusion: 该工作为ITrees提供了首个组合式时序规约框架，实现了对effectful程序的模块化时序验证，并可与既有Hoare式规约无缝组合。

Abstract: The correctness of modern effectful programs depends not only on their final results but also on the temporal evolution of observable interactions with their environments. Interaction Trees (ITrees) provide a reusable denotational semantics for such programs by representing effectful computations as potentially infinite trees of observable interactions. However, reusable temporal reasoning over ITrees remains largely unexplored. This paper presents a compositional temporal specification framework for ITrees. We introduce iBuchi, a shallowly embedded temporal specification language based on Choice Trees, which represents finite and infinite interaction traces and supports expressive temporal operators, including sequencing, branching, conjunction, repetition, and Linear Temporal Logic modalities. We define trace- and tree-level satisfaction relations between iBuchi specifications and ITrees, together with compositional reasoning principles for modular verification of effectful programs. We integrate iBuchi into the Dijkstra Monads Forever framework, thereby enabling temporal specifications to compose with conventional Hoare-style specifications and supporting a generator-based encoding of future conditions. We have formally developed and verified our framework in Rocq and demonstrated its expressiveness and compositionality through several non-trivial case studies.

</details>

### [1] [System Capybara: Tracking Capabilities for Separation and Freshness (Extended Version)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09383)
*Yichen Xu,Oliver Bračevac,Cao Nguyen Pham,Yaoyu Zhao,Martin Odersky*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出Capybara系统，在Scala的capture checking基础上选择性添加别名控制（分离、消费、新鲜性、只读访问），通过类型保持翻译到核心演算CoreCapybara，并在Lean 4中证明了语义可靠性（类型安全、内存安全、数据竞争自由等），最终实现了Scala 3的分离检查器，将高阶分离推理引入普通Scala代码。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 如何将子结构类型系统（唯一性、分离、借用）的强别名控制引入高阶抽象、无限制别名和普遍共享的既有语言（如Scala），且无需全局约束，仅选择性增强需要严格别名控制的代码。

Method: 基于Scala的capture checking机制（能力为普通值，capture集记录值可用的能力），设计System Capybara，添加选择性别名控制层（分离、消费、新鲜性、只读访问）。给出从表层演算Capybara到核心演算CoreCapybara的类型保持翻译，使用量词实现capture多态和新鲜性，使用约束索引模态类型实现分离。

Result: 在Lean 4中证明了核心演算的语义可靠性，包括类型安全、内存安全（无use-after-free或double-free）、只读计算的不变性和无数据竞争。实现了Scala 3的分离检查器，支持高阶分离推理。

Conclusion: 通过选择性而非全局的方式，成功将子结构和所有权式别名控制引入现有主流语言，使普通代码保持原有别名规则的同时，在需要的地方强制执行更强的别名保证，并支持无惧并发。

Abstract: Substructural type systems give strong static control over aliasing. Examples include uniqueness, separation, and borrowing. How can such control be brought to established languages whose programming models rely on higher-order abstraction, unrestricted aliasing, and pervasive sharing? We study this problem in the context of Scala. We show how to retrofit these guarantees selectively instead of globally: ordinary code keeps Scala's usual aliasing discipline, while stronger guarantees can be enforced where they matter.
  Our starting point is Scala's capture checking, whose treatment of capabilities is inspired by the object-capability tradition: capabilities are ordinary values, and capture sets record, in a value's type, which capabilities the value may use. We develop System Capybara, which adds a selective alias-control layer to this mechanism. By tracking separation, consumption, freshness, and read-only access for capabilities, Capybara recovers key reasoning principles from substructural and ownership-based disciplines without global invariants.
  We give a type-preserving translation from the surface calculus Capybara to CoreCapybara, a core calculus extending System Capless, the earlier foundation for capture checking. The translation uses quantifiers for capture polymorphism and freshness, and constraint-indexed modal types for separation. We prove a semantic soundness result for the core calculus in Lean 4 and derive type safety, memory safety (no use-after-free or double-free), immutability of read-only computations, and data-race freedom for well-typed programs.
  Finally, we implement Scala 3's new separation checker, which brings higher-order separation reasoning about effects, capabilities, and resources to ordinary Scala, including fearless concurrency.

### [2] [Bidirectional Elaborators à la Carte](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09564)
*Andrew Slattery,Jonathan Sterling*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于依赖类型单子领域特定语言（DSL）的方法，用于可执行地规范正确性有保障的 elaboration 算法。该 DSL 抽象于具体的正规形式表示和转换检查算法，通过浅嵌入双向类型化的表面语言，将表面项到核心项的翻译简化为基本的等式计算，确保翻译不会产生类型错误的项，并自动在核心项的判定等性和替换下保持稳定。最终通过预层模型从该 DSL 中代数地提取具体的 elaboration 算法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 证明助手（如 Rocq、Lean、Agda、Idris）的表面语法高度隐式，缺少将用户代码映射到精确定义数学对象所需的细节。Elaboration 算法负责将这些隐式信息补充完整，将表面语法翻译为显式的核心语法。然而，elaboration 的可靠性和可预测性依赖于核心类型系统的若干关键性质（如判定等式的可判定性、类型构造子的单射性），这些依赖关系在具体系统中表现为显式调用转换检查和弱头归约子例程。现有实现缺乏一种抽象、可组合且正确性有保障的方式来规范 elaboration 算法。

Method: 作者引入了一个依赖类型的单子领域特定语言（DSL），用于可执行地规范正确性有保障的 elaboration 算法。该 DSL 抽象于任何具体的正规形式表示或转换检查算法。他们通过浅嵌入在该 DSL 中表示 Martin-Löf 类型理论的双向类型化表面语言，使得表面项到核心项的翻译本质上等价于基本的等式计算。翻译的正确性由构造保证：不会产生类型错误的项，并自动在核心项的判定等性和替换下保持稳定。从后一性质中，他们获得了 elaboration 问题挂起的新的指称解释。最终，通过基于 Martin-Löf 类型理论的双初始自然模型构建的预层模型的代数手段，从 DSL 中提取出具体的 elaboration 算法。

Result: 1. 提出了一个依赖类型单子 DSL，可抽象地规范正确性有保障的 elaboration 算法。2. 通过浅嵌入实现了表面语言到核心语言的翻译，该翻译正确性由构造保证（不会产生类型错误的项）。3. 证明了翻译自动在核心项的判定等性和替换下保持稳定。4. 从替换稳定性中获得了 elaboration 问题挂起的新的指称解释。5. 通过基于双初始自然模型的预层模型，代数地提取出具体的 elaboration 算法。

Conclusion: 该工作为证明助手中 elaboration 算法的正确性提供了一个抽象、模块化的框架，将 elabration 的规范与具体的表示和算法实现分离。通过使用依赖类型单子 DSL 和预层模型，实现了从抽象规范到具体算法的正确性有保障的提取，为未来在更丰富的类型理论中构建可靠的 elaboration 算法奠定了基础。

Abstract: Surface syntax in proof assistants like Rocq, Lean, Agda, and Idris is highly implicit, lacking many details that are needed for user-written code to denote precisely defined mathematical objects. Elaboration is an algorithm that accounts for these details by translating surface syntax to an explicit enough core syntax. The reliability and predictability of elaboration relies on several critical properties of the core type system, including decidability of judgemental equality and the injectivity of type constructors; these dependencies are witnessed in a concrete system by explicit calls to conversion checking and weak-head reduction subroutines.
  We introduce a dependently typed monadic domain specific language for the executable specification of correct-by-construction elaboration algorithms that is abstracted from any particular representation of normal forms or algorithm for conversion checking. In particular, we represent a bidirectionally typed surface language for Martin-Löf type theory by shallow embedding in this DSL so that the translation of surface terms into core terms amounts to elementary equational calculation. This translation is correct by construction in the sense that it cannot produce ill-typed terms, and is automatically stable under judgemental equality of core terms and even under substitution; from the latter property, we obtain a new denotational interpretation of the suspension of elaboration problems. Finally, a concrete elaboration algorithm is extracted by algebraic means from a presheaf model of the DSL built out of the bi-initial natural model of Martin-Löf type theory.

### [3] [Quantum Orchestras: a Concrete Semantics for Recursive Hybrid Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09605)
*Alex Rice,Dominik Leichtle,Kim Worrall,Robert I. Booth*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种名为"量子乐团单子"(quantum orchestra monad)的通用方法，用于为支持中途测量和非终止的混合量子编程语言构建指称语义。该单子基于量子仪器形式化，作用于DCPO范畴，能够处理发散程序，并自然扩展了经典状态单子和概率幂域单子。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的混合量子编程语言（通过引用寻址量子比特、量子操作突变外部量子状态）的语义尚未得到充分发展，尤其是当语言允许中途测量和非终止时，缺乏通用的指称语义构建方法。

Method: 定义量子乐团单子(quantum orchestra monad)，基于量子信息论中的量子仪器(quantum instruments)形式化，作用于DCPO范畴以处理非终止情况。

Result: 量子乐团单子能够准确捕捉混合量子编程中的量子效应，自然扩展了经典状态单子和概率幂域单子，并揭示了在非交换量子情况下简单扩展这些定义时存在的微妙问题。

Conclusion: 该工作为含中途测量和非终止的混合量子编程语言提供了构建指称语义的通用方法，量子乐团单子是这一框架的核心工具。

Abstract: Many production quantum programming languages represent hybrid quantum computations by extending a classical base language with a quantum effect, where qubits are addressed by reference, and quantum operations are understood to mutate some external quantum state. However, the semantics of this view of quantum computation remains underdeveloped, especially when the language allows mid-circuit measurements and non-termination.
  In this work, we provide a general method for building denotational semantics for such languages, by defining the quantum orchestra monad, which precisely captures this style of quantum effect. The monad has a concrete presentation, being based on the formalism of quantum instruments, a common tool in quantum information theory for capturing the action of a quantum process along with its classical outcomes. It acts on the category DCPO, and so enables the interpretation of divergent hybrid programs.
  The quantum orchestra monad serves as a natural extension of both the classical state monad and the probabilistic powerdomain monad. We investigate some of the subtleties present when trying to naïvely extend these definitions to the quantum non-commutative case.

### [4] [Kleene Algebra with Transitive Commutativity Conditions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09635)
*Han Xu,Chenyu Zhou,Zachary Kincaid,David Walker*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文完整解决了Kleene代数在交换性条件C下的等式理论可判定性问题，证明其可判定当且仅当C是传递的，并进一步强化了双向结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Kleene代数(KA)是程序结构和控制流推理的基础代数框架。Kozen在1996年提出KA可以扩展交换性条件C（即形如ab=ba的等式），但哪些关系C能使KA+C的等式理论可判定一直是一个悬而未决的问题。早期正则语言的结果表明可判定当且仅当C传递，但KA+C的情况更为复杂，直到最近Kuznetsov(2023)才解决了首个非平凡情形，然而整体可判定性问题仍保持开放。

Method: 作者从两个方向完整刻画了KA+C等式理论的可判定性：1) 负面方向：当C不传递时，证明KA+C的全称性问题已经不可判定；2) 正面方向：当C传递时，证明KA*+C（带交换性的Kleene代数星）与KA+C的等式理论是等价的，从而将KA+C的可判定性归约到已知可判定的KA*+C问题。

Result: 完整解决了KA+C等式理论的可判定性问题，给出了精确的充要条件：KA+C的等式理论可判定当且仅当交换性关系C是传递的。当C不传递时，即使是更弱的全称性问题也是不可判定的；当C传递时，KA*+C与KA+C的等式理论等价，因此可判定。

Conclusion: 本文彻底解决了Kleene代数在交换性条件C下的等式理论可判定性问题，填补了自Kozen(1996)提出该问题以来近三十年的理论空白。该结果不仅与正则语言在交换性条件下的可判定性刻画相一致，还通过双向强化展示了问题的完整轮廓。

Abstract: Kleene algebra (KA) provides a foundational algebraic framework for reasoning about program structure and control flow. To capture equivalences arising from reordering or independence of actions, Kozen [1996] purposed that KA can be extended with commutativity conditions, that is, equations of the form { ab = ba | (a,b) \in C }, where C is a binary relation on constant symbols. This paper studies the following question: for which relations C is the equational theory of KA+C decidable?
  Early related work [Bertoni et al. 1982; Ibarra 1978] showed that regular languages modulo commutativity conditions C are decidable if and only if C is transitive. For Kleene algebra KA and commutativity conditions C, however, the situation is substantially more difficult. Only very recently, Kuznetsov [2023] showed that the equational theory of Kleene algebra KA+C is undecidable under certain specific commutativity conditions, settling the first nontrivial cases more than 25 years after the corresponding problem for KA* +C was resolved by Kozen [1996]. Nevertheless, the decidability problem of KA+C remained open.
  In this work, we resolve this question completely by showing that the equational theory of KA+C is decidable if and only if C is transitive. Moreover, we strengthen the result in both directions. On the negative side, we show that when C is not transitive, the universality problem for KA+C is already undecidable. On the positive side, we show that for transitive C, the equational theories of KA* +C and KA+C coincide.

</details>

Abstract: Network engineers often need to perform network diagnosis and inference tasks, which frequently require answers to enumeration questions such as "Which packets from the Internet arrive at host C?" or "Which single-link failures disconnect my network?" Parametric NetKAT is a new domain-specific language that combines elements of NetKAT, Relational NetKAT, and Weighted NetKAT into a single system and extends them with parameters, allowing users to pose such enumeration questions directly over network models. This paper presents the design and semantics of Parametric NetKAT and illustrates its utility through a series of examples.

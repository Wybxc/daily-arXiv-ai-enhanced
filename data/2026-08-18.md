### [1] [Beyond Single-Vulnerability Evaluation: Closing the Engineering Decision Gap Between C Retrofits and Native Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15486)
*Andrew Laramore,Joseph Spracklen,Murtuza Jadliwala*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种新的评估范式，将Rust和Go等原生内存安全语言与组合部署的C语言内存安全加固机制进行基准比较。研究发现，多层C语言防御会产生叠加且依赖于工作负载的性能惩罚，存在根本性的架构不兼容问题，且保护范围仍不及原生内存安全语言。作者主张内存安全研究应从孤立评估转向整体性、比较性的框架。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 过去数十年的C语言内存安全改造研究几乎都针对特定漏洞类别进行孤立评估，导致实践者无法了解组合部署多层防御时的累积性能开销、互操作性冲突和保护盲区。这种零散的评估范式使工程师在"改造遗留代码"与"迁移到安全语言"之间做出决策时缺乏充分依据。

Method: 提出一种新的评估范式，使用标准化跨语言任务（cross-language tasks），将Rust和Go等原生内存安全语言与组合部署（layered/compounded）的C语言内存安全加固机制进行对照基准测试，评估其在组合使用时的性能表现和保护能力。

Result: 研究表明：多层C防御产生叠加且随工作负载变化的性能惩罚；不同加固机制之间存在根本性的架构不兼容问题；组合后的C防御在保护范围上仍无法达到原生内存安全语言的水平。

Conclusion: 当前孤立评估范式掩盖了向C回填安全性的真实成本，形成了一个关键的工程决策盲区。作者主张内存安全研究应转向整体性、比较性的评估框架，以支持在改造遗留代码库与迁移至现代安全语言之间做出公正、知情的高风险决策。

Abstract: While decades of research have produced numerous retrofitted memory-safety protections for C, these mechanisms are almost exclusively evaluated in isolation, targeting specific vulnerability classes. This siloed evaluation paradigm leaves practitioners without a clear understanding of the cumulative performance costs, interoperability conflicts, and protection gaps that arise when layering defenses to achieve comprehensive safety. This paper presents a new evaluation paradigm that benchmarks natively memory-safe languages like Rust and Go against compounded C retrofits. Using standardized cross-language tasks, we evaluate the performance and protection tradeoffs of state-of-the-art mechanisms when deployed in combination. Our results demonstrate that layered C defenses incur compounding and workload-dependent performance penalties, can suffer from fundamental architectural incompatibilities, and fall short of the protection scope provided by native memory-safe languages. These findings expose a critical engineering decision gap where the true cost of backporting safety to C remains hidden from practitioners. We argue for a fundamental shift in memory-safety research: moving away from isolated evaluation toward holistic, comparative frameworks that inform the high-stakes choice between retrofitting legacy codebases and migrating to modern, safe languages.

### [2] [Type-Directed Discretization of Probabilistic Programs (Extended Version)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16093)
*Katherine Wu,Jules Jacobs,Kevin Batz,Alexandra Silva*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出一种名为Slice的精确离散化变换，用于递归高阶概率程序（含连续分布），通过类型导向的非局部分析将连续值划分为有限个可观测相关区域，使精确推断转化为离散问题，并可与Dice、Roulette、Storm等离散推断引擎协同执行。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 连续分布的递归高阶概率程序的精确推断非常困难，而离散概率程序已有成熟的精确推断引擎（如Dice、Roulette、Storm）。当程序中连续值仅与有限多个常数比较时，理论上可以转化为离散问题，但缺乏系统的语义保持变换方法，已有精确系统无法处理此类挑战性连续程序。

Method: 提出Slice变换，核心是一种非局部、类型导向的分析：推断连续值在何处可以被划分为有限个观测相关区域，然后重写这些区域上的采样和比较行为。由于该构造是全局且类型导向的，正确性验证需要超越局部语法推理：作者通过操作语义上的耦合式逻辑关系论证（coupling-style logical relations）形式化并证明了该变换对布尔查询的可靠性。

Result: 实验评估表明Slice与离散后端配对时具有两个互补优势：(1) 能够对超出此前精确系统能力范围的挑战性连续程序进行精确推断；(2) 在可直接比较的基准测试上，与最先进的连续程序精确推断系统具有竞争力。

Conclusion: Slice提供了一种通用的、可证明正确的精确离散化方法，弥合了连续与离散概率程序精确推断之间的鸿沟，扩展了精确推断的适用范围，同时保持了与现有离散推断引擎的兼容性和竞争力。

Abstract: We study exact discretization as a semantics-preserving transformation for recursive, higher-order probabilistic programs with continuous distributions. We target programs where continuous values are compared against finitely many constants, so exact inference reduces to a discrete problem. Our central technical contribution is a non-local, type-directed analysis that infers where continuous values can be partitioned into finitely many observationally relevant regions, then rewrites sampling and comparison behavior over those regions. We call this transformation Slice. Because this construction is global and type-directed, correctness requires reasoning beyond the local syntax: we formalize the transformation and prove soundness for boolean queries using a coupling-style logical relations argument over operational semantics. As an application, transformed programs can be executed by discrete engines such as Dice, Roulette, and Storm. Our empirical evaluation shows two complementary strengths of Slice when paired with discrete backends: it enables exact inference for challenging continuous programs that lie beyond the reach of previous exact systems, and, on benchmarks where direct comparison is possible, it is competitive with state-of-the-art exact inference systems for continuous programs.

</details>


### [3] [Mechanizing Choreographic Programs and Hoare Logic with State Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16346)
*Timon Böhler,Simon Daniel,David Richter,Pascal Weisenburger,Mira Mezini*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出并形式化了基于状态转换器模型的编舞编程语言，在Lean证明助手中实现了该语言的机械化和验证，包括端点投影的可靠性与完备性、死锁自由、合流性以及Hoare逻辑。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 编舞编程的形式化工作通常需要处理绑定与替换等繁琐的局部操作问题，这些与技术核心（分布式编程特定操作）无关。作者希望规避这些繁琐问题，聚焦于编舞的分布式本质方面，从而获得更简洁、更聚焦的机械化实现。

Method: 作者将Thiemann提出的状态转换器模型（用于在依赖类型语言中优雅地建模无死锁进程）引入编舞编程中。状态转换器表示每个进程执行的计算，从而抽象掉局部语言细节，减少绑定与替换相关的机械化工作。在Lean中实现了支持点对点通信、广播、递归过程以及局部有状态方法（每个参与者可被分配不同方法集）的编舞语言。

Result: 作者在Lean中成功机械化了一门支持点对点通信、广播、递归过程及局部有状态方法的编舞语言，并证明了以下性质：端点投影的可靠性与完备性、投影进程的死锁自由、合流性，以及编舞Hoare逻辑的成立。

Conclusion: 通过状态转换器模型，编舞编程的形式化工作得以简化，将繁琐的绑定与替换问题从核心分布式语义中剥离出去。该工作证明了在依赖类型证明助手中可以优雅地机械化编舞语言，并同时获得多项关键安全性质的形式化保证。

Abstract: Choreographic programming is a programming model for developing distributed applications where an entire communication protocol is written as a single program, which a compiler then projects to one process per participant. Choreographic programming abstracts over low-level network communication primitives such as sockets, and provides a high degree of safety guarantees with deadlock freedom ensured by construction. Mechanizing choreographies necessarily deals with both operations specific to distributed programming and standard (local) operations that also occur in non-distributed programs, as well as the typical issues of binding and substitution. We aim to sidestep the latter issues, thereby obtaining a more concise mechanization that focuses on the essential distributed aspects of choreographies. To this end, we use a method recently proposed by Thiemann to elegantly model deadlock-free processes in a dependently typed language: Using state transformers to represent the computations performed by each process. We bring the state transformer model to choreographies, allowing us to reduce the usual mechanization effort around binding and substitution, and to abstract over the details of the "local" aspects of the language. We mechanize in Lean a choreographic language that supports point-to-point communication, broadcasting, recursive procedures, and local stateful methods, allowing each participant to be assigned a different set of methods. We prove soundness and completeness of endpoint projection, establish deadlock freedom for the projected processes, prove confluence, and verify a Hoare logic for choreographies.

</details>

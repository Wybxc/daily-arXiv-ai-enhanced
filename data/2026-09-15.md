### [1] [Modeling Quantum Computing with $π$-calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13156)
*Catalin Toma,Manuel Oriol*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出 PiQuant 框架，将量子程序视为并发程序而非顺序程序，用通道广播量子变量状态，是 π-演算的扩展。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量子计算对计算机科学家而言难以理解：充满无直观意义的数学公式，其操作语义与常规编程语言（变量、赋值、按值传递）相距甚远。作者希望退一步，从更贴近程序员直觉的并发视角建立理论框架。

Method: 提出 PiQuant 框架，作为 π-演算的简单扩展，增加了用于处理量子变量的额外语法与语义。其核心思想是不把量子程序当作顺序程序，而是当作并发程序：变量通过各自的通道对外"通告"其状态，使用这些变量的代码则在相应通道上读取该状态（并可能使其坍缩）。

Result: 给出了 PiQuant 的语法与语义定义，为量子程序提供了一种并发式的理论建模方式，使量子计算的操作语义更接近程序员熟悉的形式。

Conclusion: 通过将量子程序重新解释为并发程序并扩展 π-演算，PiQuant 为理解与形式化量子计算提供了一条更直观的路径。

Abstract: Quantum computing is a difficult concept to grasp for computer scientists because it is full of mathematical formulas that have no intuitive meaning related to the problem that the code solves. While these formulae define the way the system behaves, the operational semantics is very far from regular programming languages that reason with variables, assignments, and passing by value the results of computation. This article takes a step back to define a theoretical framework where quantum programs are not treated as sequential programs, but rather as concurrent programs where variables advertise their states through their own channels and code that uses them is reading that state (and possibly collapsing it).
  The PiQuant framework is a simple extension of the $π$-calculus that contains additional syntax and semantics for the handling of quantum variables.

### [2] [Verification of a Generational Garbage Collector](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13186)
*Shengyi Wang,Kathrin Stark,Andrew W. Appel*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文在Rocq+VST中形式化验证了一个用C语言编写、支持可变引用且兼容OCaml数据类型的分代垃圾回收器，其精心规约的API类似于OCaml回收器，并可用于C程序或CertiRocq编译的程序。通过对客户端程序的验证证明了API规约的充分性，且程序与验证均具备模块化特性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为支持C程序及经过验证的编译器（CertiRocq）所生成代码的内存管理需求，需要一个经过形式化验证、支持可变引用且与OCaml数据类型兼容的垃圾回收器及其规约。

Method: 在Rocq（原Coq）与VST（Verified Software Toolchain）中，用C语言实现并形式化验证一个分代垃圾回收器；同时设计独立于实现的API规约，并对客户端（mutator）程序进行验证以证明规约的充分性。

Result: 成功验证了一个兼容OCaml数据类型、支持可变引用的分代回收器；其API规约被证明足以支撑客户端程序的验证，且各模块（如转发函数）的规约与验证相互独立。

Conclusion: 该回收器的API规约独立于具体实现策略（复制式vs标记清除、分代vs非分代），各实现组件的验证也相互独立（不依赖于老年代设计、多线程或记忆集等决策），从而实现了良好的模块化与可复用性。

Abstract: We have formally verified in Rocq+VST a multi-generation collector with support for mutable references, written in C and compatible with OCaml data types. Its carefully specified API supports C programs or CertiRocq (a verified compiler from Rocq to C) is similar to that of OCaml's collector. We have demonstrated the adequacy of our API specification for the mutator (client of the garbage collector) by verifying client programs. Our program and our verification are modular so that (1) the API spec is independent of the implementation (e.g., the choice of copying vs. mark-and-sweep, generational-vs-nongenerational) and (2) the specification and verification of components of the implementation (such as the forwarding function) are independent of other components (e.g., design decisions regarding older generations, multiple threads, or "remembered sets" of mutable references).

### [3] [Equality saturation theory exploration `a la carte](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14527)
*Anjali Pal,Brett Saiki,Ryan Tjoa,Cynthia Richey,Amy Zhu,Oliver Flatt,Max Willsey,Zachary Tatlock,Chandrakana Nandi*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出 Enumo，一种用于可编程理论探索的领域特定语言，通过核心算子引导重写规则推断、增量构建规则集，并提出无需目标语言求值的 fast-forwarding 策略，在多个领域合成出优于现有方法的规则集。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 重写规则对等式饱和至关重要，但高质量规则集开发困难且易错。现有自动推断方法无法扩展到大型项或文法，且工具呈单体式、不透明，用户难以引导推断与增量构建规则集，导致多数用户仍手工维护规则集。

Method: 提出 DSL Enumo，提供少量核心算子以策略性引导规则推断并增量构建规则集；算子可组合，便于开发新的规则集推断策略。引入 fast-forwarding 策略，无需对目标语言中的项进行求值，从而覆盖此前工作无法涉及的领域。此外仅需两个新算子即可将大语言模型纳入规则推断，与引导式搜索互补。

Result: 在多个领域评估 Enumo 与 fast-forwarding。与最先进技术相比，Enumo 能在多样领域合成更好的规则集，某些情况下达到与人工开发规则集在等式饱和驱动系统中相当的效果。简短程序即可复现 Ruler 等工具的结果，且能从更大文法推断更深的规则。

Conclusion: Enumo 以可编程、可组合的方式解决了规则推断的可扩展性与可引导性问题，fast-forwarding 扩展了适用领域，且易于集成大语言模型，为等式饱和中规则集构建提供了有效且可扩展的新途径。

Abstract: Rewrite rules are critical in equality saturation, an increasingly popular technique in optimizing compilers, synthesizers, and verifiers. Unfortunately, developing high-quality rulesets is difficult and error-prone. Recent work to automatically infer rewrite rules does not scale to large terms or grammars. Users struggle to guide inference and incrementally construct rulesets because existing rule inference tools are monolithic and opaque. As a result, most equality saturation users still manually develop and maintain rulesets.
  This paper proposes Enumo, a new domain-specific language for programmable theory exploration. Enumo provides a small set of core operators that enable users to strategically guide rule inference and incrementally build rulesets. Short Enumo programs easily replicate results from state-of-the-art tools like Ruler, but Enumo programs can also scale to infer deeper rules from larger grammars than prior approaches. Enumo's composable operators even facilitate developing new strategies for ruleset inference. We introduce a new fast-forwarding strategy which does not require evaluating terms in the target language, and thus supports domains that were out of scope for prior work. Enumo is also easy to extend: two new operators suffice to incorporate large language models into rule inference, where they complement guided search.
  We evaluate Enumo and fast-forwarding across a variety of domains. Compared to state-of-the-art techniques, Enumo can synthesize better rulesets over a diverse set of domains, in some cases matching the effects of manually developed rulesets in systems driven by equality saturation.

### [4] [Opportunistic ZGC: Leveraging Idle Cores for More Effective Concurrent Garbage Collection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15558)
*Jacob Malloy,Michael R. Jantz,Terry Jones*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出OppZGC——一种反馈驱动的ZGC调度策略，在CPU空闲时动态约束堆大小，无需逐应用调优即可显著降低最大堆占用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ZGC默认调度策略保守，倾向将堆增长至最大上限后再触发回收，以最小化回收开销；但当最大堆未与真实工作集匹配时，会造成内存浪费甚至损害性能，且需要人工调优。

Method: OppZGC识别CPU核心未被充分利用的时间窗口，利用这些空闲算力执行并发回收，从而动态、自动地收紧堆大小；在OpenJDK HotSpot JVM中实现该调度器。

Result: 在DaCapo Chopin与SPECjbb基准（含延迟敏感负载）上评估：DaCapo最大堆占用平均降低61%至90%（依配置而定），吞吐量与请求延迟相对默认ZGC影响极小。

Conclusion: OppZGC在不需逐应用调优的前提下，可在CPU容量充足时限制堆使用、在额外回收会显著损害性能时避免触发，实现内存占用与性能的良性折衷。

Abstract: Managed language runtimes often provide concurrent garbage collectors so that latency-critical applications with large working sets can keep running while most collection work proceeds in the background. ZGC is a production-quality, generational, concurrent collector in OpenJDK with sub-millisecond pause times. While ZGC is designed to run concurrently, frequent and excessive collections with ZGC can still slow the mutators due to synchronization costs and interference with shared computing resources. Hence, the ZGC scheduler is conservative by default, and in most cases, will grow the heap toward the maximum allowed before scheduling a collection. While this approach minimizes collection effort, it can be wasteful, or even harmful, if the maximum heap size is not well tuned to the actual working set.
  We propose Opportunistic ZGC (OppZGC), a feedback-directed ZGC scheduling policy that constrains the heap dynamically and automatically, without per-application tuning. OppZGC identifies periods when CPU cores are underutilized and leverages them for concurrent collection with ZGC. We describe the design and implementation of OppZGC in OpenJDK's HotSpot Java VM and evaluate it with standard and latency-sensitive benchmarks from DaCapo Chopin and SPECjbb. OppZGC limits heap usage when there is CPU capacity sufficient for additional collections, and avoids scheduling extra collections when they would substantially degrade performance. Overall, it reduces maximum heap usage for our DaCapo benchmarks by between 61% and 90%, on average, depending on configuration, with minimal impact on throughput and request latency compared to default ZGC.

</details>

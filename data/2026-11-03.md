*Peter Bartholomew,Luke Phillips*

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自2018年Excel计算引擎为支持动态数组而大规模升级以来，财务建模技艺发生了一系列重大变化；然而实践中仍有大量建模者沿用旧范式，未能利用新引擎能力构建更简洁、更易审计的模型，因此有必要系统梳理并推广新方法。

Method: 对比分析性研究：以传统电子表格财务建模技术为基准，展示其可被基于动态数组的全新方法所替代，并从可读性、性能与灵活性三个维度论证新方法在结构组织与公式构造上的差异。

Conclusion: 随着Excel引擎能力的大幅增强，财务建模者应转向以动态数组为核心的新建模范式，将简洁性与可审计性作为后续建模的基本准则。

Abstract: Since the calculation engine of Excel was the subject of a major upgrade to accommodate Dynamic Arrays in 2018, there has been a series of seismic changes to the art of financial modelling. This paper will show the traditional financial modelling techniques for spreadsheets can be replaced by radically different approaches that have significant gains in readability, performance and flexibility. With so much power added to the Excel engine in recent years, it is the responsibility of financial modellers to ensure that they are building in the simplest and most auditable way possible going forward.

### [2] [Grassmann: The Coordinate-Independent Linear Algebra Programming Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00182)
*Theadora Fields Collin,Gilbert Bernstein,Oded Stein,Saman Amarasinghe*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出 Grassmann 语言，用依赖类型、线性类型与部分（伪）可逆计算在流形上一点处建模外代数与微分几何，通过坐标语言在无坐标与数组表示间转换，并证明操作语义在任意坐标选择下与指称语义一致，生成的外代数运算代码开销不超过 10%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 科学计算实践中，用户先用坐标无关的线性代数表述算法（可组合、不易误用），却因 NumPy 等工具缺乏坐标无关表示而过早地手工翻译成坐标相关数组代码；而现有支持此类构造的语言又不足以建模真正需要用到的线性代数场景。作者希望弥合坐标无关线性代数与坐标相关实现之间的鸿沟。

Method: 设计小型语言 Grassmann：(1) 使用依赖类型、线性类型和部分（伪）可逆计算来建模外代数与微分几何中的情形，聚焦于流形上一点处的线性代数；(2) 内置坐标语言，使用户能在坐标无关表示与坐标相关表示之间切换；(3) 给出无坐标的指称语义，以及在给定坐标选择下将 Grassmann 翻译为数组代码的操作语义。

Result: 主元定理表明：在任意坐标选择下，操作语义的求值结果与指称语义一致，从而形式化刻画了实践者将线性代数翻译给计算机的过程；基于该语言的优化实现可为外代数运算生成代码，额外开销不超过 10%。

Conclusion: Grassmann 为连接抽象线性代数与具体数组实现提供了形式化基础，其语义等价性保证了坐标选择不影响语义正确性，并提示了在高抽象层次进行算法优化的可行路径。

Abstract: Scientists, engineers, and mathematicians often formulate algorithms using coordinate-independent linear algebra because its tools are composable and harder to misuse. For numerical processing, however, users must translate these ideas into coordinate-dependent array code. Most practitioners do so prematurely because tools such as NumPy lack coordinate-independent representations, while existing languages with such constructs do not model enough linear algebra for the situations where they matter. We propose Grassmann, a small language bridging coordinate-independent linear algebra and coordinate-dependent implementations. It uses dependent types, linear types, and partially (pseudo-)invertible computation to model situations arising in exterior algebra and differential geometry. It targets the linear algebra at a point on a manifold, a prerequisite for many specifications in scientific computing and computer graphics. A language for coordinates lets users move between these representations. We give a denotational semantics assigning meaning without coordinates and an operational semantics translating Grassmann to array code under a choice of coordinates. Our main metatheoretic result shows that operational evaluations agree with the denotational semantics under an arbitrary choice of coordinates. This models how practitioners translate linear algebra for computers and suggests an approach to algorithmic optimization at a high level of abstraction. Our optimized implementation generates code for exterior algebra operations with overheads no more than 10%.

### [3] [Stochastic Summaries: Characterizing Structured Datasets Using Probabilistic Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01189)
*Jack Stanek,Adithya Murali*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出概率编程语言 Tali，用于学习归纳数据类型的“随机摘要”，并基于 Observation-Bounded Compilability 属性结合程序合成与最大似然推理学习此类程序。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大规模数据的表示与分析需要同时刻画数据结构性与统计性属性的摘要，现有方法难以统一；树状归纳数据类型的摘要学习在编程语言领域尤具价值，但缺乏可精确推理且表达力足够的表示形式。

Method: 定义基于归纳数据类型的概率编程语言 Tali；提出语义性质 Observation-Bounded Compilability（OBC），刻画可进行精确推断的递归概率程序子集；设计结合程序合成（随机搜索）与最大似然推断的学习算法，从数据样本中学习满足 OBC 的 Tali 程序，并实现学习框架。

Result: 在多样化基准测试集上评估学习框架，记录方法优劣；验证学习到的随机摘要可作为符号化先验用于下游学习任务，并可用于学习真实世界数据集的操作画像（operational profiles）。

Conclusion: 概率程序可作为树状归纳数据类型随机摘要的有效表示，OBC 为精确推断提供了可判定的语义边界，所提学习框架在实践中具有多样化的实用价值。

Abstract: What does my data look like? We study the problem of representing and learning summaries of datasets that account for both their structural and statistical properties. This problem is widely useful across many application domains that involve large datasets and analysis/modeling of data, and is of particular interest to Programming Languages. In particular, in this work we focus on the problem of learning these "stochastic summaries" for tree-like data composed of inductive datatypes and argue that probabilistic programs over inductive datatypes can effectively represent the desired summaries. We present Tali, a Probabilistic Programming Language over inductive datatypes, and study the problem of learning such programs from data samples. We identify a semantic property, called Observation-Bounded Compilability, which identifies a set of recursive probabilistic programs which admit exact inference, and can represent many useful and complex probabilistic structures. We then propose an algorithm for learning Tali programs satisfying the OBC property from observations using a combination of program synthesis (stochastic search) and maximum likelihood inference. We implement our learning framework, evaluate it on a diverse benchmark suite, and record our observations on the merits of our approach. We demonstrate the utility of learning stochastic summaries as symbolically-represented priors for downstream learning tasks. We also showcase another utility by using our technique to learn operational profiles of real-world datasets.

### [4] [MBUndo: Sound, Automatic, and Efficient Measurement-Based Uncomputation for Quantum Circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02345)
*Shengyuan Yang,Charles Yuan*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出 M\**Undo，一个自动将基于酉门的 ancilla 反计算转换为基于测量的反计算（MBU）的编译器 pass，通过对 ancilla 进行静态分析确定测量合法位置，并用电路变换把酉反计算门化为测量后的相位修正，在 22 个基准上实现超过 36.5% 的 T 门削减。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 使用 ancilla 的量子程序必须显式反计算以恢复零态；MBU 用廉价测量加相位修正替代昂贵的酉 T 门，可显著降低成本。但现有框架仅能对 Toffoli 等特定门用测量反计算，缺少能把任意「标准计算-反计算」电路自动转为 MBU 的通用变换，研究人员只能手工逐设计应用，限制了算法开发与编译器优化。该变换缺失的根因是两个未知：MBU 在何处适用，以及任意计算下哪些门可实现 MBU。

Method: 1) 静态分析：对每个 ancilla 比特识别所有测量合法的电路位置，超越「MBU 仅在电路末尾适用」的基本假设；2) 电路变换：将酉反计算门可靠地转换为测量后的高效相位修正，推广了此前仅限单门或不适用于一般电路的改写规则；3) 集成为编译器 pass M\**Undo，仅需用户标注哪些比特为 ancilla（与现有反计算综合器相同），并可增强其输出电路。

Result: 在 22 个基准上评估，相较反计算综合器实现大于 36.5% 的 T 门削减。

Conclusion: M\**Undo 首次实现任意电路的自动化测量反计算转换，无需额外用户标注，可提升现有反计算综合器的输出质量并降低 T 门开销，从而缓解手工 MBU 应用对算法开发与编译器优化的限制。

Abstract: Quantum programs that use temporary values, known as \emph{ancilla} qubits, must explicitly \emph{uncompute} each ancilla back to zero to ensure that the program produces correct outputs. Conventional uncomputation uses unitary quantum gates, whereas leading quantum algorithms now leverage \emph{measurement-based uncomputation} (MBU), which replaces costly unitary $T$ gates with cheaper measurements and phase corrections.
  Current quantum programming frameworks can use measurement to uncompute specific gates such as Toffoli. No automatic transformation, however, accepts an arbitrary circuit whose ancillas are computed and uncomputed via the standard approach and converts the circuit to use MBU\@. Researchers instead apply MBU to individual circuit designs by hand, which limits algorithm development and compiler optimization.
  Such a transformation has not existed due to two fundamental unknowns: where MBU applies and which gates realize MBU for an arbitrary computation. To solve these, we first present a static analysis that identifies, for each ancilla qubit, each circuit position where measurement is sound --- subsuming basic assumptions that MBU only applies at the end of a circuit. Next, we present circuit transformations that soundly turn unitary uncomputation gates into efficient phase corrections after measurement --- generalizing rewrite rules from prior literature that are either limited to a single gate or not applicable to a general circuit.
  We present \textsf{MBUndo}, an integrated compiler pass that automatically converts circuits to measurement-based uncomputation. It requires no user annotations other than which qubits are ancillas, the same as needed by existing uncomputation synthesizers, and can enhance their output circuits. We evaluate \textsf{MBUndo} on 22 benchmarks to reveal \emph{T} gate reductions of ${>}36.5\%$ over uncomputation synthesizers.

### [5] [Vectorizing Recurrences over Semirings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02451)
*Yihong Zhang,Bala Vinaithirthan,Karima Ma,Zachary Tatlock,Maaz Bin Safeer Ahmad,Andrew Adams*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出半环上递推的代数框架及向量化恒等式，实现编译器VecRec，将递推程序编译为高效向量化C++，最高获得一个数量级加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 递推在信号/图像处理、生物信息学、数值计算、组合优化等领域普遍存在，但因看似本质串行，常被串行实现，无法利用现代硬件的向量指令，导致大量本可并行的硬件资源被闲置。

Method: 建立半环上递推的代数（recurrence algebra），研究可支持向量化的代数恒等式；基于该框架给出多种向量化变换，并自然推广到二维递推与相互递推（mutual recurrences）；在编译器VecRec中实现该代数与变换，将递推程序编译为向量化C++核函数。

Result: 生成的核函数在多种递推上相对串行实现最高达一个数量级（约10倍）加速；生物信息学案例研究表明其与手工向量化的现有库具竞争力，且在许多情况下更优。

Conclusion: 代数化方法可系统地将表面串行的递推转化为可向量化形式，验证了框架的通用性与实用性，为递推的自动向量化提供了可行途径。

Abstract: Recurrences are ubiquitous in signal and image processing, bioinformatics, numerical computing, combinatorial optimization, and many other domains. Because recurrences seem inherently sequential, they are often implemented serially and thus do not leverage vector instructions in modern hardware. This leaves much of the available, often highly parallel, hardware underutilized.
  In this paper, we present an algebra of recurrences over semirings and study algebraic identities that enable vectorization. Using our algebraic framework, we present a number of vectorizing transformations that yield efficient, vectorized implementations across various settings. These transformations naturally extend to 2D recurrences and mutual recurrences.
  We implement the recurrence algebra and the vectorizing transformations in a compiler called VecRec.VecRec compiles recurrence programs to efficient vectorized C++ implementations. The generated kernels can achieve up to an order-of-magnitude speedup over serial implementations across a wide variety of recurrences. A case study in bioinformatics shows that they are competitive with, and in many cases better than, existing libraries with manually-crafted vectorization.

### [6] [Functional Logic Programming: Improving Logic Programs with Ease](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03177)
*Michael Hanus*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该文提出一种将谓词与目标系统化转换为函数与嵌套表达式的变换，通过按需求值与失败考虑，使转换后程序的执行步数不多于原程序，最好情况下可将无限搜索空间缩减为有限。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 逻辑编程基于关系定义，函数通常仅被视为可转换为谓词的语法糖，其逻辑不被用于计算目的。作者希望说明函数的使用能改善逻辑程序的操作行为，同时不损失逻辑编程的灵活性。

Method: 给出从谓词到函数、从目标到嵌套表达式的系统化转换；采用需求驱动（按需）方式求值函数，并考虑潜在失败，从而保证转换后程序语义不变。

Result: 转换后程序的执行所需步数不会多于原程序，且可能减少步数；最优情形下可将无限搜索空间归约为有限搜索空间。

Conclusion: 函数式逻辑编程为在不改变语义的前提下改善逻辑程序操作行为提供了合适基础；从操作角度看，相较基于关系的逻辑编程具有明显优势。

Abstract: Logic programming is based on defining relations. Functions are often considered as syntactic sugar which can be transformed into predicates so that their logic is not used for computational purposes. In this paper, we show that the use of functions improves the operational behavior of logic programs without loosing the flexibility of logic programming. For this purpose, we present a systematic transformation from predicates and goals into functions and nested expressions, respectively. By evaluating functions in a demand-driven manner and taking potential failures into account, it is ensured that the execution of the transformed programs will never require more steps than the original programs but can decrease the number of steps---in the best case reducing infinite search spaces to finite ones. Thus, functional logic programming provides an adequate basis to improve the operational behavior of logic programs without changing their semantics. This also shows that, from an operational point of view, functional logic programming has clear advantages compared to relation-based logic programming.

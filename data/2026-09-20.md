### [1] [Splyce: SIMD Vectorization of Sparse Coiteration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19410)
*Kabilan Mahathevan,Poorna Gunathilaka,Kirshanthan Sundararajah*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: Splyce 是一个基于 MLIR 的自动向量化框架，通过双路径执行模型将稀疏-稀疏协同迭代中的坐标求交与指针管理解耦，以选择性谓词化消除数据依赖分支，从而提升指令级并行与功能单元利用率，在稀疏张量内核上取得 1.96X–2.86X 加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 稀疏张量收缩的核心瓶颈在于稀疏-稀疏协同迭代循环，其控制流数据依赖强、难以进行标准循环向量化。现有方法无法在保持压缩存储内存效率的同时充分利用现代超标量架构的执行单元吞吐。

Method: 提出 MLIR 自动向量化框架 Splyce，采用双路径执行模型：通过选择性谓词化将坐标求交与指针管理解耦，从而在副效应上天然消除数据依赖分支；同时暴露可并发执行的独立计算，缓解顺序依赖对功能单元利用率的限制。

Result: 在基础稀疏张量内核上，合成输入取得 1.96X–2.86X 性能提升；在 SuiteSparse 集合中绝大多数不规则真实数据集上保持稳定加速。

Conclusion: Splyce 表明，将不可预测的控制流转化为可预测的数据流，编译器驱动的推测执行能够有效兼顾压缩存储的内存效率与现代超标量架构的执行单元吞吐。

Abstract: Sparse tensor contractions are bottlenecked by sparse-sparse coiteration loops that resist standard loop vectorization. We present Splyce, an auto-vectorization framework in MLIR that overcomes this through a dual-path execution model. By decoupling coordinate intersection from pointer management via selective predication, Splyce inherently eliminates data-dependent branches as a side effect, allowing modern superscalar engines to maximize instruction-level parallelism and hide memory latency. Beyond simple branch elimination, our transformation exposes independent computation that can be executed concurrently, increasing functional-unit utilization that would otherwise be constrained by sequential dependencies. Evaluation across foundational sparse tensor kernels demonstrates performance ranging from 1.96X to 2.86X on synthetic inputs, with consistent speedups sustained across a vast majority of irregular real-world datasets from the SuiteSparse collection. Ultimately, Splyce demonstrates that by converting unpredictable control-flow into a predictable data stream, compiler-driven speculation can effectively reconcile the memory efficiency of compressed storage with the execution-unit throughput of modern superscalar architectures.

### [2] [LLVM Translation Validation Automated with Large Language Models and Lean](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19583)
*Chunhao Liao,Hongxu Xu,Xintong Zhou,Yizhou Zhang,Chengnian Sun*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: Trivet结合大语言模型与Lean定理证明器，实现LLVM变换的自动化翻译验证，在148个变换中验证或反驳147个，并优于无脚手架基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLVM的IR语义微妙，变换易于出错，需形式化验证。现有工具Alive2虽成功但存在可扩展性限制、不支持符号位宽、对循环仅有有界保证；而交互式定理证明器（如Lean）虽能处理这些情形，却需要大量证明工程。

Method: Trivet基于源函数与目标函数生成结构化证明脚手架，自动完成适合确定性推理的义务，并将变换特有的义务委托给LLM。输出精化证明或基于反例的反驳，且每个成功结论均由Lean内核检查。

Result: 在148个LLVM变换上，Trivet验证或反驳了147个，仅1个无效案例未解决。成功案例包括60个带符号位宽的无循环变换、27个来自受限类别的含循环变换，以及10个Alive2超时的复杂有效固定位宽案例。相比无脚手架基线，脚手架额外支持26个证明；在两者均解决的案例上，平均证明时间减少75.9%，平均费用减少88%。

Conclusion: Trivet将LLM与Lean结合，可扩展LLVM翻译验证的能力，覆盖Alive2难以处理的符号位宽与循环场景，并通过Lean内核保证结果可靠性，同时显著提升效率与降低成本。

Abstract: LLVM is the cornerstone of modern compilers, but its subtle intermediate representation (IR) semantics make transformations error-prone and necessitate formal verification. Alive2, a state-of-the-art translation validator based on satisfiability modulo theories, has achieved substantial success in automating the validation of LLVM transformations. However, it still faces scalability limitations, does not support symbolic bitwidths, and offers only bounded guarantees for loops. In contrast, interactive theorem provers such as Lean can address these cases but require substantial proof engineering. In this paper, we present Trivet, a framework combining large language models (LLMs) and Lean for automated translation validation of LLVM transformations. Trivet generates structured proof scaffolds based on source and target functions, automatically discharges obligations amenable to deterministic reasoning, and delegates transformationspecific obligations to LLMs. It produces refinement proofs or counterexample-based refutations, with every successful verdict checked by the Lean kernel. On 148 LLVM transformations, Trivet verifies or refutes 147, leaving one invalid case unresolved. Successful cases include 60 loop-free transformations with symbolic bitwidths, 27 cases from a restricted class of loop-containing transformations, and 10 complex valid fixed-bitwidth cases on which Alive2 times out. Compared with an unscaffolded baseline, scaffolding enables 26 additional proofs. On cases solved by both configurations, it reduces mean proof time by 75.9% and mean monetary cost by 88%.

### [3] [Theories of Mind as Domain-Specific Languages of Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19598)
*Kartik Chandra,Jonathan Ragan-Kelley,Joshua B. Tenenbaum,Rebecca Saxe*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该文提出将"心智理论"形式化为领域专用的编程语言，以memo语言为例说明其如何刻画直觉心理学推理，并借此探讨心智理论的三个理论问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 心智理论究竟是什么这一问题长期缺乏精确的形式刻画，作者希望借助"思维即内部思维语言编程"的经典思想，以可执行的形式化框架澄清心智理论的结构与运作方式。

Method: 不再设定单一通用的思维语言，而是提出一族领域专用的思维语言，其语法与语义编码各领域的理论；在直觉心理学领域将其实例化为真实编程语言memo，该语言通过"knows""wants""thinks"等领域专用句法构造支持心智理论推理，并通过一系列实例分析展示其刻画能力。

Result: 通过将心智理论形式化为memo式编程语言，作者在三个理论问题上取得洞见：(1)单一固定的直觉理论如何应对结构变化无界的社会情境；(2)模块化、领域专用且信息封闭的社会认知系统如何与外部的世界知识及其他认知系统(如直觉物理)交互；(3)儿童与成人心智理论的差异、发展过程中理论增长所获得的内容及其发生机制。

Conclusion: 将心智理论类比并形式化为领域专用编程语言，为心智理论研究提供了统一且精确的分析框架，有助于解释社会推理的灵活性、认知系统的接口结构以及心智理论的发展变化。

Abstract: What kind of thing is a ``theory of mind''? We propose to formalize theories of mind as domain-specialized programming languages, which can be used to reason about the mental states of other agents. Our proposal builds on the longstanding idea that thinking is akin to programming in an internal ``language of thought.'' But rather than positing a single general-purpose language for all of thought, we posit a collection of domain-specific languages of thought, whose respective syntaxes and semantices encode the theories of various domains. We make this idea concrete in the domain of intuitive psychology using a real-world programming language called memo, which is specialized for theory-of-mind reasoning via domain-specialized syntactic constructs like ``knows,'' ``wants,'' and ``thinks.'' In our view, using one's theory-of-mind to think about a social situation is analogous to using memo to write a program that models that situation. Through a series of worked examples, we show how formally representing a theory of mind as a memo-like programming language can lend insight into a variety of theoretical issues in theory-of-mind research: (1) how a single, fixed intuitive theory can be used to reason about social situations with unbounded structural variation; (2) how a modular, domain-specialized, and informationally-encapsulated system for social cognition might interface with external world knowledge, as well as other cognitive systems like intuitive physics; and (3) how different theories of mind, such as those of children and adults, differ, what exactly is gained as theories grow over the course of development, and how that growth might occur.

### [4] [The Output-Space Hypothesis: Enumerative Equivalence Checking for Tensor Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19611)
*Paul Biberstein,Joseph Devietti,Mayur Naik*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出 Dirigo 系统，通过翻转量词（对单个输出位置验证所有输入）与符号执行，在 6,988 个被差分测试判定正确的 AI 生成 CUDA kernel 中发现 600 个实际有 bug 的内核，97.3% 的 bug 在两分钟内定位。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 张量程序是深度学习优化的重点目标，但优化易引入隐蔽 bug。传统差分测试基于随机输入与参考实现对比，正确性只是默认假设；由于输入为大规模张量，触发 bug 往往需要构造概率极低、且张量元素间存在精确关系的输入，导致 bug 难以稳定复现。

Method: 核心思想是翻转量词：不再"生成单个输入、检查所有输出张量位置是否等价"，而是"针对单个输出张量位置，验证其是否对所有输入均等价"。系统 Dirigo 基于一种新颖的符号执行策略实现该思想。

Result: 在一个包含 6,988 个 AI 编写 CUDA kernel 的公开数据集上（这些 kernel 均被差分测试标记为正确），Dirigo 发现其中 600 个内核实际存在 bug，且 97.3% 的 bug 可在两分钟内被检出。

Conclusion: 通过量词翻转与符号执行，Dirigo 能比传统差分测试更稳定、更高效地发现张量程序中的深层 bug，表明随机差分测试的"正确"结论并不充分，所提方法对张量程序正确性验证具有实用价值。

Abstract: Tensor programs, as used in deep learning models, are a prime target for optimization, as small performance improvements can have a large impact across training or inference workloads. However, such optimizations are complicated and can produce subtle bugs. Traditionally, correctness is assumed when differential testing against a reference on random inputs fails to reveal bugs. However, the inputs to these programs are massive tensors, and finding bugs can require generating extremely low likelihood inputs with precise relationships among their values.
  We propose a novel way to find bugs more consistently by flipping the quantifiers. Rather than generating a single input and checking all output tensor locations for equivalence, what if you could check a single output tensor location's equivalence for all inputs? We implement this idea in a system, \dirigo, by using a novel symbolic execution strategy. We demonstrate that \dirigo can find bugs effectively in a public dataset of 6,988 AI-written CUDA kernels that are all marked correct by differential testing. Of these, \dirigo finds 600 kernels that are actually buggy, and finds 97.3\% of those bugs within two minutes.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 提出一种蜕变测试方法，采用语义保持的重写规则，重点关注浮点特殊值和窥孔优化，对SMT求解器的浮点公式处理进行性能测试，并使用真实测试输入。

Using real-world test inputs, our approach is able to identify for every solver tested SMT queries for which solving time increases when the queries are simplified; we see slowdowns of up to 33.4x for Z3, 5.6x for MathSAT, 4.5x for cvc5, and 1.8x for Bitwuzla. We further observe that the approach is less successful when using input files that were randomly generated from a grammar.

### [6] [How Often Does Your Program Fail?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20037)
*Arnab Ray,Aalok Thakkar*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 该论文提出一个统一框架，将符号方法与统计方法作为特例整合，用于分布感知的可靠性估计，保证返回包含真实触发率的置信区间。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 软件在生产中会遭遇由实际使用方式塑造的输入分布，因此需要分布感知的可靠性估计：给定程序、运行输入分布和关注条件，确定该条件成立的概率并以有保证的误差界认证结果。符号方法可精确认证比率但难以处理复杂算术或循环；统计方法适用性广但稀有事件需大量采样。

Method: 提出一个统一框架，将符号方法与统计方法都作为特例。每个估计器由三部分组成：质量估计器（mass estimator）、逐叶置信界（per-leaf confidence bound）和符号闭包规则（symbolic closure rule）。证明任何满足三条不变量的实例化都能以置信度1-delta返回包含真实触发率的区间，无论何时停止。

Result: 认证误差包含两部分：统计项（通过采样降低）和结构项（通过符号闭包降低）。纯采样和纯符号执行各自只能降低其中一项。通过替换不同组件，该框架还能支持稀有事件方差缩减方法。

Conclusion: 该工作为分布感知可靠性估计提供了统一的、带保证的框架，兼具符号方法的认证能力与统计方法的广泛适用性，并可扩展至稀有事件方差缩减。

Abstract: Software in production encounters inputs shaped by how it is used in practice. We study distribution-aware reliability estimation: given a program, its operational input distribution, and a condition of interest, determine how often that condition holds and certify the result with a guaranteed error bound.
  Symbolic and statistical methods offer two ways to answer this question. Symbolic methods reason about entire regions of the input space and can certify rates exactly, but often struggle with complex arithmetic or loops. Statistical methods instead sample inputs and apply concentration bounds. They are broadly applicable, but can require many samples when failures are rare. We bring these approaches together in a framework that includes both as special cases. Each estimator has three components: a mass estimator, a per-leaf confidence bound, and a symbolic closure rule. We prove that any instantiation satisfying three invariants returns an interval containing the true rate with confidence 1-delta, regardless of when it stops. The certified error has two parts: a statistical term, reduced by sampling, and a structural term, reduced by symbolic closure. Pure sampling and pure symbolic execution each reduce only one of these terms. Alternative choices of the components also allow the framework to support rare-event variance-reduction methods.

</details>

### [1] [pfoqcompiler: Efficient Compilation of Quantum Control in Recursive First-Order Quantum Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08503)
*Mário Silva,Alexandre Guernut,Marco Lewis*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 提出面向一阶量子编程语言FOQ、targeting Qiskit的编译器pfoqcompiler，通过利用程序结构合并量子分支间兼容的递归过程调用，实现多项式规模、多对数深度的电路。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 递归量子控制虽能简洁描述算法，但朴素电路编译会将量子if语句各分支串行化，导致电路深度指数增长；现有并行化技术则以指数级辅助量子比特为代价。目标是在不付出该代价的前提下并行化分支。

Method: 设计pfoqcompiler编译器，利用程序结构在量子分支之间合并兼容的递归过程调用；同时提出更弱的、可语法检查的良基性（well-foundedness）、递归减半（recursive halving）与一致性（uniformity）条件，并保持原有的复杂度刻画。

Result: 对弱良基、有界宽度程序，编译器生成多项式规模电路；弱递归减半程序进一步获得多对数深度；弱一致性消除了分支串行化的渐近开销。

Conclusion: 借助更弱的语法条件与跨分支递归调用合并，pfoqcompiler在保持既有复杂度刻画的同时，实现了无指数辅助比特开销的分支并行编译。

Abstract: Recursive quantum control allows for simple and concise descriptions of algorithms. However, a naive circuit compilation sequentializes the different branches of the quantum if-statement and results in an exponential increase in circuit depth. Existing techniques can parallelize these branches, but do so at the cost of an exponential number of ancillary qubits. We present pfoqcompiler, a compiler targeting Qiskit for the first-order quantum programming language FOQ that exploits program structure to merge compatible recursive procedure calls across quantum branches. We introduce weaker syntactically-checkable conditions for well-foundedness, recursive halving, and uniformity, while preserving previously demonstrated complexity characterizations. For weakly well-founded bounded-width programs, the compiler produces polynomial-size circuits; weakly recursively halving programs additionally yield polylogarithmic depth, while weak uniformity removes the asymptotic overhead of branch sequentialization.

### [2] [A Complete Calculus for Hamiltonian Quantum Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08675)
*Jacques Carette,Chris Heunen,Robin Kaarsgaard,Neil J. Ross,Amr Sabry*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 论文提出 Hamilton——一种并行算子即"同时作用的相互作用之和"的进程演算，其操作语义为 Lie-Trotter 乘积公式，将程序精度减半并展开为电路实现编译；语义完全抽象，可表达哈密顿量恰为 Pauli 系数形如 m/2^k 者，等式理论完备且可判定，并有完整实现与 Agda 机器验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量子计算通过演化哈密顿量来模拟物理系统、求解优化问题、估计特征值，连流行的电路抽象最终也在硬件上编译为哈密顿量。然而缺乏一个以哈密顿量为原语、具有严格语义与可判定等价性的编程/演算模型，用以推理并行相互作用的组合与编译正确性。

Method: 引入 Hamilton 进程演算，其并行算子解释为多个相互作用同时发生之和。操作语义采用 Lie-Trotter 乘积公式：把和恢复为越来越精细的顺序交错之极限。编译即按给定精度对程序反复减半（halving），并将交错展开为量子电路。作者证明语义的完全抽象性，刻画可表达哈密顿量类（Pauli 系数为 m/2^k），给出完备且可判定的等式理论；实现将 Hamilton 程序编译为电路并判定等价性，核心元理论以 Agda 形式化验证。

Result: 语义完全抽象（fully abstract）；可表达的哈密顿量恰为 Pauli 系数形如 m/2^k 者，与逐次减半所达精度一致；等式理论完备且可判定；完整实现可将 Hamilton 程序编译为电路并判定其等价性；Agda 证明对核心元理论进行了校验。

Conclusion: Hamilton 为以哈密顿量为原语的量子编程提供了具备严格语义基础、可判定等价性与机器验证元理论的演算框架，把 Lie-Trotter 公式这一数值近似提升为操作语义，并明确了可精确表达的计算类，为量子程序的编译与等价性推理奠定基础。

Abstract: Given the forces acting on a quantum particle, a Hamiltonian models its continuous evolution. Quantum computers evolve Hamiltonians to simulate physical systems, to solve optimisation problems, and to estimate eigenvalues, and even the popular circuit abstraction is compiled to Hamiltonians on the hardware. We introduce Hamilton, a process calculus whose parallel operator is the sum of interactions acting simultaneously. Its operational semantics is the Lie-Trotter product formula, which recovers a sum as the limit of ever finer sequential interleavings; compiling a program means halving it to a chosen precision and unrolling the interleaving into a circuit. The semantics is fully abstract, the expressible Hamiltonians are exactly those whose Pauli coefficients have the form $m/2^k$, the precision that successive halving reaches, and the equational theory is complete and decidable. A full implementation compiles Hamilton programs to circuits and decides their equivalence, and Agda proofs sanity-check the core metatheory.

### [3] [Fibered Separation Logic: Weakest Preconditions, Universal Constructions, and Local Independence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08685)
*John M. Li,Ryan Doenges,Pedro H. Azevedo de Amorim*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出高阶分离逻辑（含最弱前置条件模态）模型的范畴论框架，用纤维化上的强单子解释最弱前置条件，引入"束纤维化"概念并证明其等价于BI超 doctrines，统一点sound地刻画了常见启发式技巧，并基于LIP范畴构造出新的概率分离逻辑。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有高阶分离逻辑缺乏统一的范畴语义框架来统一处理最弱前置条件模态、帧规则"烘焙"等启发式技巧，且概率分离逻辑的语义构造冗长晦涩。作者希望用范畴论给出原则化、可组合的语义基础，并推广到名字生成、随机性、局部状态等效果。

Method: 在Moggi计算lambda演算模型的纤维范畴上，用强单子解释最弱前置条件；定义"束纤维化"（bunched fibration）并证明其与BI超doctrines等价；将最弱前置条件的启发式技巧范畴化并证明其可靠性；证明"烘焙帧规则"满足某个泛性质；给出添加cost credits的构造；以Simpson的局部独立积（LIP）范畴为参数，构造分配单子、束纤维化与单子提升。

Result: 证明束纤维化与BI超doctrines等价；将常见最弱前置条件技巧形式化并证其可靠性；证明"baking in the frame rule"满足泛性质；提出扩展程序逻辑以支持cost credits的构造；由LIP范畴系统性导出分配单子、束纤维化与单子提升，并实例化为一个新颖的测度论概率分离逻辑，简化并澄清了已有工作。

Conclusion: 该范畴论框架为高阶分离逻辑提供了统一、原则化的语义，统一了启发式技巧并给出通用构造recipe；LIP范畴作为统一来源可同时涵盖名字生成、随机性与局部状态，所得概率分离逻辑更简洁清晰，展示了框架的表达力与可扩展性。

Abstract: This paper introduces a categorical framework for models of higher-order separation logic including the weakest precondition modality. Weakest preconditions are interpreted by strong monads on a category fibered over a model of Moggi's computational lambda calculus. The fibered categories in question are termed bunched fibrations and shown to be equivalent to BI hyperdoctrines.
  Well-known heuristic techniques for obtaining weakest preconditions are shown to be expressible in categorical terms and are proved sound. The ``baking in the frame rule'' trick is shown to satisfy a universal property. Constructions are given for extending a program logic with cost credits. Our largest case study is parametrized by a category having local independent product (LIP) structure, a notion due to Simpson that axiomatizes name generation, randomness, and local state. From an LIP category, we construct an allocation monad, a bunched fibration, and a monad lifting. We instantiate our construction to obtain a novel probabilistic separation logic for measure-theoretic probability that both simplifies and clarifies prior work.

### [4] [Nothing Is Shared Until You Synchronise: Reinterpreting Java for Data-Race Freedom](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09410)
*Luke Cheeseman,Elias Castegren,David Black-Schaffer,Erik Österlund,Tobias Wrigstad*

Main category: cs.PL

TL;DR: 本文提出 Sync-as-Share 内存模型，以线程隔离和显式通信取代传统“共享内存+同步排序”范式：线程读写私有视图，同步动作同时承担通信职责，从而在构造上消除数据竞争，并在同步点检测冲突；在 Java 上实例化为版本控制内存并形式化验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 主流共享内存语言依赖锁等同步机制仅建立时间序（temporal ordering），但共享内存仍在每次普通读写时在线程间传播更新。程序员必须理解“排序与通信”的关系并正确一致地施加同步，而编译器与硬件却假定其正确性进行优化；同步不当即丧失隔离性，程序语义在无提示情况下改变，甚至产生数据竞争。作者希望把“隔离”与“通信”作为一等概念，而非从访问排序推导语义。

Method: 1) 以线程隔离为起点：每线程读写私有内存视图。2) 重新解释程序既有的同步动作，使其显式承担更新传播（push/pull）职责；非同步访问不通信，故数据竞争按构造不可能发生。3) 冲突在同步点检测而非普通访问处。4) 从“通信什么、发给谁、何为冲突”的设计空间提出两种通信拓扑（trade-off 不同）。5) Java 实例化：仅以 Java 内存模型已定义的同步动作作为通信点，程序无需修改；形式化为“版本控制内存”（version-controlled memory），并在 Rocq 中机械化验证。

Result: 在 Java 内存模型下正确同步的 Java 程序，在共享内存语义与 Sync-as-Share 语义下行为完全一致（可观察行为相同）；正确同步程序无需修改即可采用新模型；错误同步的程序不再产生未定义的数据竞争语义，而是以同步点冲突检测暴露问题。该实例化已在 Rocq 中完成机械化形式化与证明。

Conclusion: 将同步语义从“访问排序”重构为“显式通信”是可行且向后兼容的：Sync-as-Share 在保留正确同步程序可观察行为的同时，从构造上消除数据竞争，并把隔离性与通信策略显式化为可设计、可论证的模型维度；版本控制内存为 Java 提供了具体实现路径与机器检验的形式化保证。

Abstract: In mainstream shared-memory languages, programmers use synchronisation mechanisms such as locks to isolate shared data accesses across threads to avoid data races. Yet synchronisation enforces only a temporal ordering, while shared memory still communicates updates between threads at every ordinary read and write. To achieve isolation, programmers therefore need to understand the connection between ordering and communication, and to apply synchronisation correctly and consistently, while compilers and hardware optimise assuming they have. When programmers synchronise incorrectly, they lose isolation, and the semantics of their programs change without notice.
  We propose Sync-as-Share, a memory model built on isolation and communication rather than on the ordering of accesses to shared memory. We start from thread isolation, where each thread reads and writes a private view of memory, and reinterpret programs' existing synchronisation actions to also communicate updates explicitly. As nothing is communicated outside synchronisation actions, data races become impossible by construction. Instead, conflicting updates are detected at synchronisation points. Viewing synchronisation as explicit communication exposes the design decisions of what should be communicated, to whom, and what counts as a conflict. We propose two communication topologies with different trade-offs. We instantiate Sync-as-Share for Java by making the synchronisation actions already defined by its memory model the only points at which threads communicate, so Java programs can adopt Sync-as-Share unmodified. We formalise this instantiation as version-controlled memory, in which synchronisation actions push and pull updates between private views, and mechanise it in Rocq. We show that Java programs that are correctly synchronised under the Java memory model behave identically under shared memory and Sync-as-Share.

</details>
